# Fire hazard properties

The following properties of a material or assembly that indicate how they behave under specific fire test conditions:

(a) Average specific extinction area, critical radiant flux and Flammability Index, determined as defined in Schedule 1.
(b) Smoke-Developed Index, smoke development rate and Spread-of-Flame Index, determined in accordance with Specification 3.
(c) Group number and smoke growth rate index (SMOGRARC), determined in accordance with Specification 7.
