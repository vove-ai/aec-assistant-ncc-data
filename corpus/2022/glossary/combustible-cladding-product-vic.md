# Combustible cladding product

Means—

(a) aluminium composite panels (ACPs) with a core of less than 93 per cent inert mineral filler (inert content) by mass in external cladding as part of a wall system; and
(b) expanded polystyrene (EPS) products used in an external insulation and finish (rendered) wall system.
