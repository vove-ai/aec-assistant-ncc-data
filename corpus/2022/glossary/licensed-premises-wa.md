# Licensed premises

Includes—

(a) premises in respect of which a cabaret licence as defined by the Liquor Control Act 1988 has been granted under that Act; or
(b) premises in respect of which a tavern licence, a hotel restricted licence or any other kind of hotel licence as defined by the Liquor Control Act 1988 has been granted under that Act; or
(c) a cabaret, hotel or tavern—
  (i) in respect of which a special facility licence as defined by the Liquor Control Act 1988 has been granted under that Act; and
  (ii) in respect of which paragraph (a) or (b) does not apply.
