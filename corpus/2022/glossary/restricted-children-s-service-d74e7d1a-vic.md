# Restricted children’s service

A children’s service that is—

(a) any one of the following as defined in the Children’s Services Regulations 2020—
  (i) a limited hours Type 1 service; or
  (ii) a limited hours Type 2 service; or
  (iii) a short term Type 1 service; or
  (iv) a short term Type 2 service; or
(b) an associated children's service within the meaning of the Children's Services Act 1996 approved to be operated by an approved provider at the same place as an approved education and care service that is required to meet the conditions of a limited hours Type 1 service, a limited hours Type 2 service, a short term Type 1 service, or a short term Type 2 service.
