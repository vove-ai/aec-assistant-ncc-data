# 10.6.3 — Location of sanitary compartments

**(1)** A sanitary compartment must not open directly into a kitchen or pantry unless—

(a) access is by an airlock, hallway or other room, (see Figure 10.6.3); or
(b) the sanitary compartment is provided with an exhaust fan or other means of mechanical exhaust ventilation.
