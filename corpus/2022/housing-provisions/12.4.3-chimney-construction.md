# 12.4.3 — Chimney construction

**(1)** The construction of a chimney must comply with H1D5 and the following:

(a) The walls of the chimney above the level referred to in 12.4.2(c) must be lined internally to a thickness of not less than 10 mm with composition mortar parging.
(b) The composition mortar in (a) must comply with AS 3700 or AS 4773 except that the mortar must be mixed by volume in the proportions of 1 part cement : 1 part lime : 5 parts sand.
(c) The chimney or flue must terminate not less than 300 mm above the highest part of the building within a horizontal distance of 3.6 m of the chimney or flue (see Figure 12.4.3).

![Figure 12.4.3: Section showing height and position of chimney](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2022/housing/image-12-4-3-section-showing-height-and-position-of-chimney.svg)

> **Info**
>
> - The requirements of this Part are to be read in conjunction with the building sealing requirements in Part 13.4. However, it should be noted that Part 13.4 does not apply in all States and Territories.
> - 12.4.3(a) requires the internal faces of masonry chimneys to be parged with a mortar to protect masonry elements and mortar beds from the corrosive by-products of combustion.
