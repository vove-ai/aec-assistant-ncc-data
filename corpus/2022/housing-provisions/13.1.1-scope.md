# 13.1.1 — Scope

**(1)** This Section sets out the following Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for energy efficiency:

(a) Building fabric (see Part 13.2).
(b) External glazing (see Part 13.3).
(c) Building sealing (see Part 13.4).
(d) Ceiling fans (see Part 13.5).
(e) Whole-of-home energy usage (see Part 13.6).
(f) Services (see Part 13.7).
