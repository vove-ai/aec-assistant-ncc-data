# 10.2.14 — Shower area requirements

Shower areas must be designed as either enclosed or unenclosed—

(a) to include a floor waste with falls complying with 10.2.12; and
(b) with a—
  (i) stepdown complying with 10.2.15; or
  (ii) hob complying with 10.2.16; or
  (iii) level threshold complying with 10.2.17.
