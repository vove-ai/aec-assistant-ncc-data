# 5.3.4 — Internal walls

**(1)** Where internal masonry walls intersect with other internal or external walls they must comply with the relevant provisions of this Part and be—

(a) not less than 75 mm thick; and
(b) either—
  (i) bonded at the junctions of the intersecting walls; or
  (ii) provided with an articulation joint in accordance with 5.6.8.

**(2)** Where a vertical articulation joint is provided in an internal masonry wall it must be formed in accordance with 5.6.8.
