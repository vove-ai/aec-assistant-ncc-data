# 4.2.16 — Footing slabs for Class A sites

**(1)** Footing slabs for Class A sites supporting the following external wall types must comply with Figure 4.2.16:

(a) Clad frame.
(b) Articulated masonry.
(c) Masonry veneer.
(d) Articulated full masonry.
(e) Full masonry.

![Figure 4.2.16: Footing slabs for Class A sites suitable for clad frame, articulated masonry veneer, masonry veneer, articulated full masonry and full masonry](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2022/housing/image-4-2-16-footing-slabs-class-a-sites-clad-frame-masonry.svg)

> (1) Use SL63 when slab length is less than 12 m.
> (2) Use SL62 when slab length is less than 18 m.
> (3) In parts of Western Australia (around Perth) and other locations where the site consists of extremely stable sands, and where specified by a professional engineer, the slab thickness may be reduced to 85 mm and reinforced as follows:
>   (a) Use SL53 when slab length is less than or equal to 12 m.
> (4) Dune sands may require compaction.
