# 13.7.2 — Insulation of services

**(1)** Thermal insulation for central heating water piping and heating and cooling ductwork must—

(a) be protected against the effects of weather and sunlight; and
(b) be able to withstand the temperatures within the piping or ductwork; and
(c) use thermal insulation material in accordance with AS/NZS 4859.1.

> **Info**
>
> The central heating water piping provisions apply to systems designed to heat the building via water, such as a hydronic heating system.
