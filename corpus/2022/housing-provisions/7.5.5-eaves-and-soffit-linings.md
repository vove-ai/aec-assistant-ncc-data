# 7.5.5 — Eaves and soffit linings

**(1)** Where provided, external fibre-cement sheets and linings used as eaves and soffit linings must—

(a) comply with AS/NZS 2908.2 or ISO 8336; and
(b) be fixed in accordance with Table 7.5.5 and Figure 7.5.5 using—
  (i) 2.8 × 30 mm fibre-cement nails; or
  (ii) No. 8 wafer head screws (for 4.5 mm and 6 mm sheets only); or
  (iii) No. 8 self embedding head screws (for 6 mm sheets only).
