# 10.7.8 — Acceptable forms of construction for timber and steel framed walls

**(1)** Acceptable forms of construction for timber and steel framed walls are set out in (2) and (3).

**(2)** Two rows of 90 mm x 35 mm timber studs or two rows of 64 mm steels studs at 600 mm centres with—

(a) an air gap not less than 20 mm between the rows of studs; and
(b) 50 mm thick glass wool insulation or 60 mm thick polyester insulation with a density of 11 kg/m³, positioned between one row of studs, and
(c) two layers of 13 mm fire protective grade plasterboard or one layer of 6 mm fibre cement sheet and one layer of 13 mm fire protective grade plasterboard, fixed to outside face of studs,

has an Rw + Ctr of not less than 50, if constructed in accordance with Figure 10.7.8a.

**(3)** Two rows of 64 mm steel studs at 600 mm centres with—

(a) an air gap not less than 80 mm between the rows of studs; and
(b) 200 mm thick polyester insulation with a density of 14 kg/m3 positioned between studs; and
(c) one layer of 13 mm fire-protective grade plasterboard and one layer 13 mm plasterboard on one outside face and one layer of 13 mm fire-protective grade plasterboard on the other outside face,

has an Rw + Ctr of not less than 50, if constructed in accordance with Figure 10.7.8b.
