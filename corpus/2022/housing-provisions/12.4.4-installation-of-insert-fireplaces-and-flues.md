# 12.4.4 — Installation of insert fireplaces and flues

**(1)** An insert fireplace and flue must comply with the following:

(a) The insert fireplace and flue must be—
  (i) tested and passed the tests required by AS/NZS 2918; and
  (ii) fitted into a masonry fireplace (including chimney) constructed in accordance with H1D5 and Figure 12.4.4.
(b) The flue must be double skin and have been tested and pass the tests required by AS/NZS 2918.
(c) There must be a clearance of 50 mm between the outer flue and adjacent materials.
(d) The flue must terminate in accordance with Figure 12.4.3.
(e) The hearth must be constructed in accordance with 12.4.2(b) and (d).
