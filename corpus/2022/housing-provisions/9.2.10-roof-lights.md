# 9.2.10 — Roof lights

**(1)** Combustible roof lights, skylights or the like installed in a roof or part of a roof required to have a non-combustible covering must—

(a) have an aggregate area not more than 20% of the roof or part of the roof; and
(b) be not less than—
  (i) 900 mm from the allotment boundary other than the boundary adjoining a road alignment or other public space; and
  (ii) 1.8 m from any roof light or the like in another building on the allotment other than an associated building or a detached part of the same building (see Figure 9.2.10).
