# 4.1.3 — Explanation of terms

**(1)** Figures 4.1.3a, 4.1.3b and 4.1.3c depict footing and slab members and associated terminology used to describe them in Part 4.2 of the ABCB Housing Provisions.

![Figure 4.1.3a: Footing and slab members and associated terminology: diagram 1](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2022/housing/image-4-1-3a-footing-and-slab-members-and-associated-terminology-diagram-1.svg)

> (1) Slab (monolithic).
> (2) Deepened edge beam.
> (3) Reinforcement.
> (4) Foundation.
> (5) Vapour barrier/damp-proofing membrane.
> (6) Edge rebate.
> (7) Internal beam (thickening).

![Figure 4.1.3b: Footing and slab members and associated terminology: diagram 2](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2022/housing/image-4-1-3b-footing-and-slab-members-and-associated-terminology-diagram-2.svg)

> (1) Slab.
> (2) Deepened edge beam and slab.
> (3) Reinforcement.
> (4) Foundation.
> (5) Vapour barrier/damp-proofing membrane.
> (6) Edge rebate.
> (7) Internal beam (thickening).

![Figure 4.1.3c: Footing and slab members and associated terminology: diagram 3](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2022/housing/image-4-1-3c-footing-and-slab-members-and-associated-terminology-diagram-3.svg)

> (1) Controlled fill.
> (2) Deepened edge beam and slab.
> (3) Natural ground line above cut.
> (4) Cut.
> (5) Foundation (natural ground below fill).
