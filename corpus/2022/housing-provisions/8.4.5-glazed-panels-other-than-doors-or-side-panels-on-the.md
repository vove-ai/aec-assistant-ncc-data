# 8.4.5 — Glazed panels, other than doors or side panels, on the perimeter of rooms

**(1)** All framed glazing where the lowest sight line of the glazing panel is less than 500 mm from the highest abutting finished floor level (see Figure 8.4.5) must be—

(a) Grade A safety glazing material in accordance with Table 8.4.2; or
(b) monolithic annealed glass not less than 5 mm nominal thickness provided that the area of the glazing panel is not more than 1.2 m2.
