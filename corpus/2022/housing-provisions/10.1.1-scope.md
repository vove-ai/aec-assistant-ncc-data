# 10.1.1 — Scope

**(1)** This Section sets out the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for—

(a) wet areas and external waterproofing (see Part 10.2); and
(b) room heights (see Part 10.3); and
(c) facilities (see Part 10.4); and
(d) light (see Part 10.5); and
(e) ventilation (see Part 10.6); and
(f) sound insulation (see Part 10.7); and
(g) condensation management (see Part 10.8).
