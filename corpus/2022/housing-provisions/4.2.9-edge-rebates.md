# 4.2.9 — Edge rebates

**(1)** Edge rebates for slab-on-ground and stiffened raft with masonry cavity or veneer construction must comply with the following:

(a) The rebate must not be less than 20 mm.
(b) The edge rebate must be flashed and drained in accordance with H2D4 and where it cannot be flashed, it must be filled with mortar.

> **Info**
>
> See 4.2.21 for minimum edge beam details. For single skin or framed walls with external cladding, rebates are not required.
