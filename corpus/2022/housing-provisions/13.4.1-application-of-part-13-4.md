# 13.4.1 — Application of Part 13.4

**(1)** This Part applies to—

(a) a Class 1 building; and
(b) a Class 10a building with a conditioned space.

**(2)** The provisions of (1) do not apply to the following:

(a) A building in climate zones 1, 2, 3 and 5 where the only means of air-conditioning is by using an evaporative cooler.
(b) A permanent building ventilation opening that is necessary for the safe operation of a gas appliance.

**(3)** Part 13.4 must be applied as directed in H6D2(1)(a) or (b).

> **Info**
>
> - An evaporatively cooled building in climate zones 4 and 6 must be sealed because of the likelihood of the building being heated during colder periods.
> - Appropriate ventilation requirements for gas appliances can be obtained from relevant legislation, referenced standards and product installation manuals.
