# 10.2.18 — Unenclosed showers

**(1)** Unenclosed showers must be constructed as follows:

(a) A waterstop must be installed a minimum horizontal distance of 1500 mm from the shower rose.
(b) The vertical leg of the waterstop must finish—
  (i) flush with the top surface of the floor (see Figure 10.2.18); and
  (ii) where the waterstop intersects with a wall or is joined—
    (A) the junction must be waterproof; or
    (B) the whole wet area floor must be waterproofed and drained to a floor waste as for the shower area.

**(2)** In the case of (1)(b)(ii)(B), at doorways, where the height of the tiling angle needs to be adjusted for tiling purposes, the angle must be fixed with a sealant compatible with the waterproofing membrane without damaging the waterproofing system.

> Fall is to be provided in accordance with 10.2.12.
