# 5.6.3 — Mortar mixes

**(1)** Mortar used for masonry construction must comply with AS 3700 or AS 4773 except that the mortar may be mixed by volume in the proportions stated in Table 5.6.3.

### Table 5.6.3 — Acceptable mortar mixes

| Masonry unit exposure classification | Mortar mix by volume Note 1 Cement: lime: sand | Mortar mix by volume Note 1 Cement: lime: sand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masonry unit exposure classification | General use | Suitable for concrete masonry Note 2 |
| Protected | 1:2:9 | 1:0:5 |
| General purpose | 1:1:6 | 1:0:5 |
| Exposure class | 1:0.5:4.5 | 1:0:4.2 |

> (1) Additives may be used provided they comply with the appropriate specified rate.
> (2) Mortar mixes for masonry require the use of methyl cellulose water thickener.
