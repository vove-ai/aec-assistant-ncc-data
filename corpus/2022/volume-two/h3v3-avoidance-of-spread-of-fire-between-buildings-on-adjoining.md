# H3V3 — Avoidance of spread of fire between buildings on adjoining allotments

**(1)** Compliance with H3P1(2) to avoid the spread of fire between buildings on adjoining allotments is verified when it is calculated that—

(a) a building will not cause heat flux in excess of those set out in column 2 of Table H3V3 at locations within the boundaries of an adjoining property set out in column 1 of Table H3V3 where another building may be constructed; and
(b) when located at the distances from the allotment boundary set out in column 1 of Table H3V3, a building is capable of withstanding the heat flux set out in column 2 of Table H3V3 without ignition.

> **Info**
>
> H3V3 is equivalent to C1V1 in NCC Volume One. Guidance on the use of C1V1 can be found in the Guide to NCC Volume One, and is applicable to the use of H3V3.

### Table H3V3 — Heat flux (adjoining allotment)

| Column 1 (Location) | Column 2 (Heat flux [kW/m2]) |
| --- | --- |
| On boundary | 80 |
| 1 m from boundary | 40 |
| 3 m from boundary | 20 |
| 6 m from boundary | 10 |
