# H2F2 — Weatherproofing and dampness

**(1)** A building is to be constructed to provide resistance to moisture from the outside and moisture rising from the ground.

> **Limitation**
>
> H2F2 does not apply to a Class 10 building except where its construction contributes to the weatherproofing of the Class 1 building.
