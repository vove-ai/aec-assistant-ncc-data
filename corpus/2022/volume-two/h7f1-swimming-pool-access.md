# H7F1 — Swimming pool access

**(1)** A swimming pool is to be provided with—

(a) means to restrict access to it by young children; and
(b) means to reduce the possibility of a person being entrapped or injured due to suction by a water recirculation system.

> **Application**
>
> H7F1 only applies to a swimming pool with a depth of water more than 300 mm.
