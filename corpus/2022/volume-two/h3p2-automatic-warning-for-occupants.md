# H3P2 — Automatic warning for occupants

**(1)** In a Class 1 building, occupants must be provided with automatic warning on the detection of smoke with an efficacy greater than 0.95 and a reliability greater than 0.95, so that they may evacuate in the event of a fire to a place of safety appropriate to the—

(a) function and use of the building; and
(b) occupant characteristics; and
(c) fire load and combustion characteristics; and
(d) potential fire intensity; and
(e) fire hazard.
