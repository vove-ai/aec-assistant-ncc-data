# H10O1 — Objective

**(1)** Provide, as far as is reasonable, people with safe, equitable and dignified access to a degree necessary to—

(a) buildings; and
(b) the services and facilities within.

> **Application**
>
> SA H10O1 applies to Class 1 buildings in developments of 20 or more dwellings.
