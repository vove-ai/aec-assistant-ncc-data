# H9P1 — Water use efficiency

**(1)** A building must have features that, to the degree necessary, facilitate the efficient use of potable water appropriate to—

(a) the geographic location of the building; and
(b) the available potable water supply of the building; and
(c) the function and use of the building.

> **Application**
>
> WA H9P1 only applies to Class 1 buildings, associated Class 10a buildings and swimming pools associated with a Class 1 building.
