# H9D4 — Rainwater tank overflow

**(1)** The rainwater tank must be fitted with an overflow device that disposes of overflow from the rainwater tank in accordance with—

(a) any specific requirements of the relevant authority; and
(b) Part 3.3 of the ABCB Housing Provisions.
