# C4D11 — Straining traps

**(1)** The straining trap must be connected and authorised by the Permit Authority.

**(2)** The straining pit basket must—

(a) be constructed of mild steel wire with a minimum of 2.5 mm woven to give 7 mm aperture widths on a 32 mm x 32 mm x 25 mm angle iron frame with mitred corners with a top frame of welded angle iron; or
(b) be constructed from minimum 3 mm thick mild steel plate, with 6 mm holes at 12 mm centres over the entire area of the basket; and
(c) be hot dipped galvanised after fabrication.

**(3)** If the straining trap is to be used in installations connected to the Network Utility Operator's sewerage system, it must comply with the requirements of the Network Utility Operator's guidelines for pre-treatment devices.
