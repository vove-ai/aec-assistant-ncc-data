# B3D3 — General requirements

**(1)** A non-drinking water service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1, subject to the following:

(a) Substitute the text of clause 9.7.1(a) as follows: (a) be of a type that has a removable handle.
(b) Substitute the text of clause 9.7.1(c) as follows: (c) have a non-standard inlet connecting thread and a standard hose connection outlet.
