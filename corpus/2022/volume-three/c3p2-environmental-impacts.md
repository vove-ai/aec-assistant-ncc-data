# C3P2 — Environmental impacts

**(1)** On-site wastewater management systems must protect the environment by ensuring that—

(a) surface water and ground water are not polluted: and
(b) soil productivity is maintained or enhanced; and
(c) the likelihood of contamination of soils, ground water and waterways is avoided.
