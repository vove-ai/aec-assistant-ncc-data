# B2F2 — Heated water installations

**(1)** The heated water supply must be conveyed through plumbing installations in a way that—

(a) minimises any adverse impact on building occupants, the Network Utility Operator’s infrastructure, property and the environment; and
(b) facilitates the conservation of water.
