# B2P2 — Scald prevention

**(1)** Heated water supplied by a heated water service must be delivered from the point of discharge of fixtures and appliances at a temperature which is unlikely to scald.

> **Application**
>
> B2P2 applies to fixtures and appliances used primarily for personal hygiene.
