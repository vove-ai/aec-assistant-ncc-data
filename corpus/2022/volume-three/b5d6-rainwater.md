# B5D6 — Rainwater

**(1)** Protection of drinking water services from rainwater must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1 (2018) Clause 16.4 and Table 16.4.

> **Application**
>
> B5D6 applies to scenarios not covered by S41C6(1)(b).
