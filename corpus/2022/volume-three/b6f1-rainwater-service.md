# B6F1 — Rainwater service

**(1)** A rainwater service must be designed and installed so as to operate in a way that avoids the likelihood of inadvertent contamination of any drinking water service and minimises any adverse impact on building occupants, the Network Utility Operator’s infrastructure, property and the environment.
