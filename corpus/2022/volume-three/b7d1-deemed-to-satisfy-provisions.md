# B7D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements B7P1 and B7P5B7P4 are satisfied by complying with B7D2 and B7D3.

**VIC variation (REPLACE)**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements B7P1 to B7P5 are satisfied by complying with B7D2 and B7D4.

**(2)** Where a Performance Solution is proposed, the relevant Performance Requirements must be determined in accordance with A2G2(3) and A2G4(3) as applicable.

> **Notes**
>
> There are no Deemed-to-Satisfy Solutions for B7P2 and B7P3.
