# B7F1 — Rainwater storage

**(1)** Rainwater storage systems must be designed and installed so as to operate in a way that avoids the likelihood of inadvertent contamination of any drinking water service, and minimises any adverse impact on building occupants, the Network Utility Operator's infrastructure, property and the environment.
