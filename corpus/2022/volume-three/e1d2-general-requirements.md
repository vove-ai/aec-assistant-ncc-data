# E1D2 — General requirements

**(1)** Where supply taps or other operational controls are provided in sanitary facilities for people with disability they must be in accordance with—

(a) AS 1428.1 (2001) and AS 1428.2 for passenger use areas of Class 9b and Class 10 public transport buildings; and
(b) AS 1428.1 (2009) for all other buildings.
