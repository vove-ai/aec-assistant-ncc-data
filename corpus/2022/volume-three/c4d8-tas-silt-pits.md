# C4D8 — Silt pits

**(1)** The silt pit must be connected and authorised by the Permit Authority.

**(2)** The silt pit bucket must—

(a) be constructed of mild steel plate, minimum 3 mm thick with 4 rows of 10 mm diameter holes at 25 mm centres: and
(b) be hot dipped galvanised after fabrication.
