# B2P9 — Pressure

**(1)** The points of discharge for a heated water service must—

(a) have—
  (i) a working pressure of not less than 50 kPa; and
  (ii) a static pressure within the building of not more than 500 kPa; or
(b) have water pressures suitable for the correct functioning of the fixture or appliance where water pressures are outside of (a)(i) and (a)(ii) are required.
