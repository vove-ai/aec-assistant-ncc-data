# E2F2 — Functional statement 2

**(1)** A building’s heating, cooling and/or ventilation system installation and maintenance must support energy efficient outcomes and minimise any adverse impact on building occupants or occupants of adjoining places, the Network Utility Operator’s infrastructure, property and the environment.
