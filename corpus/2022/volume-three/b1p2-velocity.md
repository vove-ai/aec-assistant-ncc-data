# B1P2 — Velocity

**(1)** Cold water service pipework must ensure that the pipework water velocity does not exceed 3 metres per second for more than 1% of the time that water is required during the annual peak hour.

> **Info**
>
> During the hour of heaviest usage expected to occur in a year, cold water service velocity of 3 metres per second must not be exceeded for 99% of the time downstream fixtures are in use.
