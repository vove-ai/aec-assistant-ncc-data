# B3F2 — Non-drinking water installations

**(1)** Non-drinking water must be supplied through plumbing installations in a way that avoids the likelihood of inadvertent contamination of any drinking water service, minimise any adverse impact on building occupants, the Network Utility Operator’s infrastructure, property and the environment.
