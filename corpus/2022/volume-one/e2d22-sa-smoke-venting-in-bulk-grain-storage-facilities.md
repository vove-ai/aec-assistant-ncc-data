# E2D22 — Smoke venting in bulk grain storage facilities

**(1)** In bulk grain storage facility—

(a) suitable natural smoke venting by fixed vents must be provided at the top of elevator towers and overhead galleries in a cell type silo; and
(b) smoke venting is not required in a storage shed.
