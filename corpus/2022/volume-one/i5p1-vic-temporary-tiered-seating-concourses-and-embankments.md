# I5P1 — Temporary tiered seating, concourses and embankments

**(1)** Temporary tiered seating stands and embankments must be designed using engineering principles and constructed to provide for the safety of patrons and orderly means of evacuation in an emergency.

> **Application**
>
> VIC I5P1 applies to all places or public entertainment as defined in the Building Act 1993 and prescribed in regulation 206 of the Building Regulations 2018.
