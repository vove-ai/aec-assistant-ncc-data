# E1P6 — Fire control centres

**(1)** Suitable facilities must be provided to the degree necessary in a building to co-ordinate fire brigade intervention during an emergency appropriate to—

(a) the function or use of the building; and
(b) the floor area of the building; and
(c) the height of the building.
