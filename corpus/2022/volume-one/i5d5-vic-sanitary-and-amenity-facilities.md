# I5D5 — Sanitary and amenity facilities

**(1)** Sanitary and amenity facilities in places of public entertainment must be provided as follows:

(a) In places other than buildings:
  (i) One closet fixture for every 200 female patrons or part thereof.
  (ii) One closet fixture or urinal for every 200 male patrons of part thereof, at least 30% of which must be in the form of closet fixtures.
  (iii) One washbasin for every 200 patrons or part thereof.
  (iv) For use by disabled persons, one unisex facility within the meaning of Part F4 of NCC Volume One for every 100 closet fixtures or part thereof required under (i) and (ii).
  (v) One drinking fountain or drinking tap for every washbasin required under (iii).
  (vi) First aid facilities in accordance with VIC F4D13.
(b) In buildings, as required to comply with Part F4.
