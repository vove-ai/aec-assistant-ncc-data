# E3D3 — Stretcher facility in lifts

**(1)** A stretcher facility in accordance with (2) must be provided—

(a) in at least one emergency lift required by E3D5; or
(b) where an emergency lift is not required, if passenger lifts are installed to serve any storey above an effective height of 12 m, in at least one of those lifts to serve each floor served by the lifts.

**(2)** A stretcher facility must accommodate a raised stretcher with a patient lying on it horizontally by providing a clear space not less than 600 mm wide x 2000 mm long x 1400 mm high above the floor level.
