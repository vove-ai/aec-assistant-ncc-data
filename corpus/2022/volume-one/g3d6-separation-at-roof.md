# G3D6 — Separation at roof

**(1)** In an atrium—

(a) the roof must have the FRL prescribed in Table S5C11g; or
(b) the roof structure and membrane must be protected by a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17.
