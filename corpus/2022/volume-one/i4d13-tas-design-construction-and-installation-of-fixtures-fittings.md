# I4D13 — Design, construction and installation of fixtures, fittings and equipment

**(1)** The provision of fixtures, fittings and equipment designed, constructed and installed in accordance with clause 4.2 and clause 4.3 of AS 4674 satisfies TAS I4P11.

**(2)** The provision of—

(a) automatic equipment that uses water to sanitise utensils or other equipment and only operate for the purposes of sanitation when the water is at a temperature that will sanitise the utensils or equipment; or
(b) a sink that meets TAS I4D12,

satisfies TAS I4P11.
