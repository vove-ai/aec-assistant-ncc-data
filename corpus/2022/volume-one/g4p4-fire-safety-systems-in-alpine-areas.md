# G4P4 — Fire safety systems in alpine areas

**(1)** A building in an alpine area must have a fire safety system installed to—

(a) facilitate fire-fighting operations; and
(b) alert occupants in the event of an emergency.

> **Application**
>
> G4P4 applies to a building constructed in an alpine area and overrules other Performance Requirements of NCC Volume One.
