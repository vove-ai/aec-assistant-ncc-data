# S5C8 — Enclosure of shafts

**(1)** Shafts required to have an FRL must be enclosed at the top and bottom by construction having an FRL not less than that required for the walls of a non-loadbearing shaft in the same building.

**(2)** The provisions of (1) need not apply to—

(a) the top of a shaft extending beyond the roof covering, other than one enclosing a fire-isolated stairway or ramp; or
(b) the bottom of a shaft if it is non-combustible and laid directly on the ground.
