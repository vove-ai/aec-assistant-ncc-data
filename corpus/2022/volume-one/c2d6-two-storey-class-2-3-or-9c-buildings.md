# C2D6 — Two storey Class 2, 3 or 9c buildings

**(1)** A building having a rise in storeys of 2 may be of Type C construction if—

(a) it is a Class 2 or 3 building or a mixture of these classes and each sole-occupancy unit has—
  (i) access to at least 2 exits; or
  (ii) its own direct access to a road or open space; or
(b) it is a Class 9c building protected throughout with a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17 and complies with the maximum compartment size specified in Table C3D3 for Type C construction.
