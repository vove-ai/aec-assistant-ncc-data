**(2)** In the formula at (1)—

(a) A_(cs) = the area of the centre region of the spandrel panel; and
(b) A_(es) = the area of the edge region of the spandrel panel, where the edge has a defined width of 127 mm; and
(c) A_(fs) = the area of the frame region of the spandrel panel; and
(d) U_(cs) = the U-value of the centre region of the spandrel panel; and
(e) U_(es) = the U-value of the edge region of the spandrel panel, where the edge has a defined width of 127 mm; and
(f) U_(fs) = the U-value of the frame region of the spandrel panel; and
(g) _= the Total System U-Value of the spandrel panel.
