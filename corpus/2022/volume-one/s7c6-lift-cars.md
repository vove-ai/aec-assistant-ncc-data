# S7C6 — Lift cars

**(1)** Materials used as—

(a) floor linings and floor coverings must have a critical radiant flux not less than 2.2; and
(b) wall and ceiling linings must be a Group 1 material or a Group 2 material in accordance with AS 5637.1.
