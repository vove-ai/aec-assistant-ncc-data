# I4D9 — Conventional stages: fire separation of stages

**(1)** A stage which is more than 50 m2 in area, and all areas below such a stage, must (with the exception of the proscenium opening) be separated from the backstage and the remainder of the building by construction having an FRL of not less than 60/60/60.
