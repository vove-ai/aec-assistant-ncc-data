# I3D6 — Thresholds

**(1)** The threshold of a doorway that serves an area not required to be accessible by D4D2 in a farm building need not comply with D3D16 where the door sill is not more than 700 mm above the finished surface of the ground, floor or the like, to which the doorway opens.
