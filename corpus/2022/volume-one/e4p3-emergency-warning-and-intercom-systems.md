# E4P3 — Emergency warning and intercom systems

**(1)** To warn occupants of an emergency and assist evacuation of a building, an emergency warning and intercom system must be provided, to the degree necessary, appropriate to—

(a) the floor area of the building; and
(b) the function or use of the building; and
(c) the height of the building.
