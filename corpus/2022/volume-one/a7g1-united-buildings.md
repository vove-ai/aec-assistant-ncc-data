# A7G1 — United buildings

**(1)** Buildings are deemed united when two or more buildings adjoining each other are connected and used as one building.

> **Application**
>
> (1) For A7G1, two or more buildings are a united building if they are connected through openings in the walls dividing them and together comply with all the requirements of the NCC as though they are a single building.
> (2) A7G1 only applies to Class 2 to 9 buildings.
