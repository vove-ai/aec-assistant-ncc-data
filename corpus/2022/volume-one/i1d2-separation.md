# I1D2 — Separation

**(1)** A theatre, public hall or the like must—

(a) have a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17; or
(b) have the stage, backstage area and accessible under stage area separated from the audience by a proscenium wall in accordance with I1D3.
