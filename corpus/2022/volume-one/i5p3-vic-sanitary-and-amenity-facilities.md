# I5P3 — Sanitary and amenity facilities

**(1)** Sufficient sanitary and amenity facilities must be provided at places of public entertainment for use by patrons.

> **Application**
>
> VIC I5P3 applies to all places of public entertainment as defined in the Building Act 1993 and prescribed in regulation 206 of the Building Regulations 2018.
