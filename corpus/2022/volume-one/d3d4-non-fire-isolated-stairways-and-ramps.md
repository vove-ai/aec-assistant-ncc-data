# D3D4 — Non-fire-isolated stairways and ramps

**(1)** In a building having a rise in storeys of more than 2, required stairs and ramps (including landings and any supporting building elements) which are not required to be within a fire-resisting shaft, must be constructed according to D3D3, or only of—

(a) reinforced or prestressed concrete; or
(b) steel in no part less than 6 mm thick; or
(c) timber that—
  (i) has a finished thickness of not less than 44 mm; and
  (ii) has an average density of not less than 800 kg/m3 at a moisture content of 12%; and
  (iii) has not been joined by means of glue unless it has been laminated and glued with resorcinol formaldehyde or resorcinol phenol formaldehyde glue.
