# S31C4 — Atrium floor protection

**(1)** The floor of the atrium must be protected by sprinklers with—

(a) the use of sidewall pattern sprinkler heads together with overhead sprinklers where dictated by the dimensions of the atrium; and
(b) sprinkler heads of the quick response type.
