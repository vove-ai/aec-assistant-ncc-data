# I4P2 — Design and construction

**(1)** The design and construction of food premises must—

(a) be appropriate for the activities for which the premises are used; and
(b) provide adequate space for the activities to be conducted on the food premises and for the fixtures, fittings and equipment used for those activities; and
(c) permit the food premises to be effectively cleaned and, if necessary, sanitised; and
(d) to the extent that is practicable—
  (i) exclude dirt, dust, odours, fumes, smoke and other contaminants; and
  (ii) not permit the entry of pests; and
  (iii) not provide harbourage for pests; and
(e) provide that the food premises is able to be used in such a manner that minimises opportunities for food contamination.
