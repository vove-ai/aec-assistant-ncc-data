# G4P1 — External doorways

**(1)** An external doorway from a building in an alpine area must be installed so that opening the door is not obstructed by snow or ice.

> **Application**
>
> G4P1 applies to a building constructed in an alpine area and overrules other Performance Requirements of NCC Volume One.
