# F1P3 — Rainwater drainage systems

**(1)** A drainage system for the disposal of surface water resulting from a storm having an annual exceedance probability of—

(a) 5% must—
  (a) convey surface water to an appropriate outfall; and
  (b) avoid surface water damaging the building; and
(b) 1% must avoid the entry of surface water into a building.
