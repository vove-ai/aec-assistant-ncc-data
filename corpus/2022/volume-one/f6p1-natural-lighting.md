# F6P1 — Natural lighting

**(1)** Sufficient openings must be provided and distributed in a building, appropriate to the function or use of that part of the building so that natural light, when available, provides an average daylight factor of not less than 2%.

> **Application**
>
> F6P1 only applies to a Class 2, 3 or 9 building, or a Class 4 part of a building.
