# F6D6 — Ventilation of rooms

**(1)** A habitable room, office, shop, factory, workroom, sanitary compartment, bathroom, shower room, laundry and any other room occupied by a person for any purpose must have—

(a) natural ventilation complying with F6D7; or
(b) a mechanical ventilation or air-conditioning system complying with AS 1668.2 and AS/NZS 3666.1; or
(c) in a storage shed or bulk grain storage facility, a ventilation system that provides one air change every six hours using openings that have a total area of the lesser of—
  (i) 35 m2; or
  (ii) one percent of the total floor area.
