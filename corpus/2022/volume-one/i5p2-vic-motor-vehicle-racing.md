# I5P2 — Motor vehicle racing

**(1)** Every place of public entertainment where motor vehicle racing takes place must be provided with suitable barriers and guard rails to protect the public from injury.

> **Application**
>
> VIC I5P2 applies to all places of public entertainment as defined in the Building Act 1993 and prescribed in regulation 206 of the Building Regulations 2018.
