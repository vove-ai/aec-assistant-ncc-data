# E2P1 — Automatic warning for sleeping occupants

**(1)** In a building providing sleeping accommodation, occupants must be provided with automatic warning on the detection of smoke so they may evacuate in the event of a fire to a safe place.

> **Application**
>
> E2P1 only applies to a Class 2, 3, 9a or 9c building or Class 4 part of a building.
