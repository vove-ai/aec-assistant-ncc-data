# F8O1 — Objective

**(1)** The Objective of this Part is to safeguard occupants from illness or loss of amenity as a result of excessive internal moisture.

> **Application**
>
> F8O1 only applies to a sole-occupancy unit of a Class 2 building or Class 4 part of a building.
