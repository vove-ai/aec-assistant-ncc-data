# S44C4 — Thermal energy load limit

**(1)** The thermal energy load limit of a space, measured in MJ/m2.annum, is calculated in accordance with the following formula:

TLL=(19.3HLL+22.6CLL−8.4)/(T_r+10.74)−15

**(2)** In the formula at (1)—

(a) TLL = the thermal energy load limit; and
(b) HLL = the heating load limit; and
(c) CLL = the cooling load limit; and
(d) T_r = the annual average daily outdoor temperature range.
