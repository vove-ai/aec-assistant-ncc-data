# I15D9 — Washing facilities

**(1)** Washing facilities served with running hot and cold water for the use of all employees engaged in a lead process must be provided consisting of—

(a) one washbasin for each 5 employees, or part thereof; and
(b) one shower bath for each 8 employees, or part thereof.
