# I4D4 — Sprinkler systems for common foyers

**(1)** In an entertainment venue, where multiple auditoriums have a foyer in common, the following applies:

(a) If the foyer serves not more than 2 auditoriums, that foyer must be separated from any adjoining foyer by construction having an FRL of not less than 60/60/60.
(b) If the foyer serves more than 2 auditoriums, a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17 must be installed—
  (i) throughout the storey containing the foyer; and
  (ii) throughout each storey in the building below that storey.
