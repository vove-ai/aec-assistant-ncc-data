# S31C19 — Emergency warning and intercom systems

**(1)** A building containing an atrium must be provided with an emergency warning and intercom system which—

(a) complies with AS 1670.4; and
(b) incorporates visual warning devices that—
  (i) operate upon the evacuation signal; and
  (ii) display the word “EVACUATE” in red with letters conforming with the requirements of the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of Part E4 for exit signs.
