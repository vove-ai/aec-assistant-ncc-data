# F7V3 — Sound transmission through floors [F7P3]

**(1)** Compliance with F7P3 to avoid the transmission of airborne and impact generated sound through floors is verified when it is measured in-situ that the separating floor has—

(a) airborne: a weighted standardised level difference (DnT,w) not less than 40 when determined under AS/NZS ISO 717.1; and
(b) impact: a weighted standardised impact sound pressure level (LnT,w) not more than 62 when determined under AS ISO 717.2.
