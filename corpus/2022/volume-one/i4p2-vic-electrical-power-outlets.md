# I4P2 — Electrical power outlets

**(1)** Sufficient general purpose outlets must be provided for electrical appliances in bedrooms in locations that obviate the need for extension leads.

> **Application**
>
> VIC I4P2 only applies to Class 3 and Class 9a residential aged care buildings and Class 9c buildings.
