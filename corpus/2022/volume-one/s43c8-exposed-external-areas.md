# S43C8 — Exposed external areas

**(1)** An external area designed to hold people unable to be safely accommodated within the building, that may be exposed to radiant heat flux from a fire front during a bushfire event, must not be exposed to an incident radiant heat flux from the fire front exceeding 1 kW/m2 above background solar radiant heat flux.
