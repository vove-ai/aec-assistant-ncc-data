# F6D12 — Kitchen local exhaust ventilation

**(1)** A commercial kitchen must be provided with a kitchen exhaust hood complying with AS 1668.1 and AS 1668.2 where—

(a) any cooking apparatus has—
  (i) a total maximum electrical power input exceeding 8 kW; or
  (ii) a total gas power input exceeding 29 MJ/hour; or
(b) the total maximum power input to more than one apparatus exceeds, per m2 of floor area of the room or enclosure—
  (i) 0.5 kW electrical power; or
  (ii) 1.8 MJ/hour gas.
