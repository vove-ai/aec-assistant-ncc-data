# I4P1 — Communication systems

**(1)** An electronic communication system must be provided to enable residents and staff to summon assistance in habitable rooms (other than kitchens), water closets, shower rooms and bathrooms.

> **Application**
>
> VIC I4P1 only applies to Class 3 and Class 9a residential aged care buildings and Class 9c buildings.
