# S2C22 — Hollow terracotta blocks

**(1)** The proportion of cored holes or perforations in a hollow terracotta block (based on the overall rectangular volume of the unit) must not exceed the following:

(a) For blocks up to 75 mm thick — 35%.
(b) For blocks more than 75 mm but not more than 100 mm thick — 40%.
(c) For blocks more than 100 mm — 50%.
