# I6D3 — Doorways to a children’s room

**(1)** A children’s room must have a doorway, or in the case of every such room accommodating more than 21 children at least two doorways as widely separated as possible, providing direct access to or from—

(a) an outdoor play area; or
(b) a passage leading to the outside; or
(c) a fire-isolated exit.
