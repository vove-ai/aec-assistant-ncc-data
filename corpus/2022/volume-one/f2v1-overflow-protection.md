# F2V1 — Overflow protection

**(1)** Compliance with F2P1 is verified when the flow rate of the in-built overflow for all vessels in the room is greater than the flow rate of the source filling the vessel.

> **Application**
>
> F2V1 only applies to bathrooms, laundries and the like in a Class 2 or 3 building or a Class 4 part of a building.
