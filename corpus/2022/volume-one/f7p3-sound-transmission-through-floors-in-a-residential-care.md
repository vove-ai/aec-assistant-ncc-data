# F7P3 — Sound transmission through floors in a residential care building

**(1)** A floor separating sole-occupancy units must minimise the transmission of airborne and impact generated sound such that the separating floor, including the effect of services and their penetrations, has—

(a) a weighted standardised level difference (DnT,w) not less than 40 for airborne sound; and
(b) a weighted standardised impact sound pressure level (LnT,w) not more than 62 for impact generated sound.

> **Application**
>
> F7P3 only applies to a Class 9c building.
