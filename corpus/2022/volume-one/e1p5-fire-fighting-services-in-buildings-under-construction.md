# E1P5 — Fire-fighting services in buildings under construction

**(1)** Suitable means of fire-fighting must be installed to the degree necessary in a building under construction to allow initial fire attack by construction workers and for the fire brigade to undertake attack on the fire appropriate to—

(a) the fire hazard; and
(b) the height the building has reached during its construction.
