# I4D37 — Provision of guardrails: fixed back seats

**(1)** If seats with fixed backs are provided, guardrails that extend for the full width of the seating, must be provided at least 500 mm above the platform unless—

(a) fixed back seats of the next lower level project at least 500 mm above the level of the stepped platform; and
(b) there is only one riser between the platform and next lower cross-over.
