# G8P2 — Hazardous conditions

**(1)** The space between buildings must be sufficient to allow access for inspection and maintenance, to avoid hazardous conditions arising due to accumulation of rubbish that could—

(a) bridge termite barriers; or
(b) harbour vermin; or
(c) create a fire hazard.
