# I4D6 — Offensive material and trade waste

**(1)** Where offensive material or trade waste is stored, a separate area or room which—

(a) is paved and easily cleanable; and
(b) is graded to drain to a suitable drainage system; and
(c) has available a supply of water under pressure,

satisfies TAS I4P5.
