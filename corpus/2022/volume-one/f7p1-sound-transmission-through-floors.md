# F7P1 — Sound transmission through floors

**(1)** A floor separating sole-occupancy units or a sole-occupancy unit from a plant room, lift shaft, stairway, public corridor, public lobby, or the like, or parts of a different classification, must minimise the transmission of airborne and impact generated sound such that the separating floor, including the effect of services and their penetrations, has—

(a) a weighted standardised level difference with spectrum adaptation term (DnT,w + Ctr) not less than 45 for airborne sound; and
(b) a weighted standardised impact sound pressure level (LnT,w) not more than 62 for impact generated sound.

> **Application**
>
> F7P1 only applies to a Class 2 or 3 building.
