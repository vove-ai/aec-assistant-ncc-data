# Building sealing

For a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2009. For Class 3 and Class 5-9 buildings, Section J of NCC 2022 does not apply and from 1 October 2023 Section J of NCC 2019 applies.
