# D1F2 — Egress

**(1)** A building is to be provided with means of evacuation which allow occupants time to evacuate safely without being overcome by the effects of an emergency.

> **Limitation**
>
> D1F2 does not apply to the internal parts of a sole-occupancy unit in a Class 2 or 3 building or Class 4 part of a building.
