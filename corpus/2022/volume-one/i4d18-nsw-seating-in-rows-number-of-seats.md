# I4D18 — Seating in rows: number of seats

**(1)** Subject to NSW I4D22, where seating is arranged in rows, the maximum number of seats in each row must not exceed—

(a) 8 where there is an aisle at one end only of the row; or
(b) 16 where there are aisles on both ends of the row.
