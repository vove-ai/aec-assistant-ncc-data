# D1F1 — Access

**(1)** A building is to provide, as far as is reasonable—

(a) safe; and
(b) equitable and dignified,

access for people to the services and facilities within.

> **Limitation**
>
> D1F1(b) does not apply to a Class 4 part of a building.
