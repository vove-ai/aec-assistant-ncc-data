# I2D7 — Stairways

**(1)** Stairs must comply with—

(a) clause 9.1 of AS 1428.1, including the notes; and
(b) clause 9.2 of AS 1428.1; and
(c) clauses 13.2, 13.3 and Figures 8 and 9 of AS 1428.2.
