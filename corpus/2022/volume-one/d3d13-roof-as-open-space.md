# D3D13 — Roof as open space

**(1)** If an exit discharges to a roof of a building, the roof must—

(a) have an FRL of not less than 120/120/120; and
(b) not have any roof lights or other openings within 3 m of the path of travel of persons using the exit to reach a road or open space.
