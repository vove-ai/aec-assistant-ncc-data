# H7P5 — Buildings in bushfire prone areas

A Class 1 building, or a Class 10a building or deck associated with a Class 1 building. that is constructed in a designated bushfire prone area must be designed and constructed to—

(a) reduce the risk of ignition from a design bushfire with an annual exceedance probability not more than 1:50 years; and
(b) take account of the assessed duration and intensity of the fire actions of the design bushfire; and
(c) be designed to prevent internal ignition of the building and its contents; and
(d) maintain the structural integrity of the building for the duration of the design bushfire. Except as amended by Planning for Bush Fire Protection.
