# H4D7 — Ventilation

**(1)** Except for an exhaust fan from a sanitary compartment, laundry, kitchen or bathroom, Performance Requirement H4P5 is satisfied for a mechanical ventilation system if it is installed in accordance with AS 1668.2.

**(2)** Performance Requirement H4P5 is satisfied for ventilation by compliance with—

(a) Part 10.6 of the ABCB Housing Provisions; or
(b) for natural ventilation, AS 1668.4.
