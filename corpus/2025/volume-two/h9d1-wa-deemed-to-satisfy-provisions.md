# H9D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Performance Requirement WA H9P1 is satisfied for a building’s water use efficiency by complying with WA H9D2.

**(2)** Performance Requirement WA H9P2 is satisfied for a building’s water loss prevention by complying with WA H9D3.

**(3)** Performance Requirement WA H9P3 is satisfied for a building’s heated water use efficiency by complying with WA H9D4.
