# WA Introduction

This Appendix contains variations and additions to the Building Code of Australia (BCA) which are necessary for its application in Western Australia. For Volume Two these apply to:

- Fire separation of roofed outdoor areas,
- Swimming pool safety barriers,
- Energy efficiency requirements for alterations or extensions to existing buildings,
- Water use; and
- The design and installation of eaves gutter.

Note: The Building Regulations 2012 in Western Australia make other modifications to the BCA. These modifications may change during a BCA cycle, and include:

- Regulation 15B - modifications to AS 1926.1-2012, and
- Regulation 15C - the dis-application of the livable (accessible) housing provisions and energy efficiency heating and cooling load limits making them voluntary.

Refer to the latest Building Regulations 2012 available from [www.legislation.wa.gov.au](https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/) for other modifications.
