# Livable housing design

This Part sets out requirements for dwellings to include features that are designed to improve their accessibility and usability for occupants and visitors, including those with a mobility-related disability.

> **Tasmania Part H8 Livable housing design**
>
> For Tasmania, refer to the Director’s Determination regarding the application of Part H8 (Livable housing provisions) of the NCC—current version available at [www.cbos.tas.gov.au](https://www.cbos.tas.gov.au/).
