# H7D4 — Construction in bushfire prone areas

**(1)** The requirements of (2) only apply in a designated bushfire prone area.

**(2)** Performance Requirement NSW H7P5 is satisfied for a Class 1 building, or a Class 10a building or deck associated with a Class 1 building, if it is constructed in accordance with—

(a) AS 3959 except as amended by Planning for Bush Fire Protection; or
(b) NASH Standard – Steel Framed Construction in Bushfire Areas except as amended by Planning for Bush Fire Protection; or
(c) the requirements of (a) or (b) as modified by development consent or a bushfire safety authority issued under Section 100B of the Rural Fires Act 1997.
