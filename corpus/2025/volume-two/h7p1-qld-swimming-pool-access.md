# H7P1 — Swimming pool access

This clause has deliberately been left blank.

Restriction of access to swimming pools in Queensland is regulated by the the Building Act 1975 (QLD), Building Regulation 2021 (QLD) and the Queensland Development Code MP 3.4 - Swimming pool barriers.
