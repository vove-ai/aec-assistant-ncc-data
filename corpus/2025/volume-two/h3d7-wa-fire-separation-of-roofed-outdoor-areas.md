# H3D7 — Fire separation of roofed outdoor areas

Compliance with WA Part 9.6 of the ABCB Housing Provisions satisfies Performance Requirements H3P1 for fire separation of roofed outdoor areas.

> For the purpose of WA H3D7 roofed outdoor areas are verandahs, alfresco dining areas, balconies and the like, and includes such uses when located below another part of the building, such as a ground floor alfresco dining area located below an upper floor verandah or balcony.
