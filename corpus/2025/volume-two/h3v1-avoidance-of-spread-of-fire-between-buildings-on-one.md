# H3V1 — Avoidance of spread of fire between buildings on one allotment

Compliance with H3P1(1)(a) to avoid the spread of fire between buildings on the same allotment is verified when—

(a) the external walls and any openings in the external walls of a building, less than 1.8 m from another building, are capable of withstanding 92.6 kW/m2 of heat flux for 60 minutes; and
(b) the external walls extend to the underside of a non-combustible roof covering or non-combustible eaves lining in accordance with clause 9.2.3 of the ABCB Housing Provisions.
