# Hours of operation

## As published in Volume One and Housing Provisions

The number of hours when the occupancy of the building is at least 20% of the peak occupancy.

## As published in Volume Two and Volume Three

The number of hours when the occupancy of the building is greater thanat least 20% of the peak occupancy.
