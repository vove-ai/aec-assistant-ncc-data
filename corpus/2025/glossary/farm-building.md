# Farm building

A Class 7 or 8 building located on land primarily used for farming —

(a) that is—
  (i) used in connection with farming; or
  (ii) used primarily to store one or more farm vehicles; or
  (iii) a combination of (i) and (ii); and
(b) in which the total number of persons accommodated at any time does not exceed one person per 200 m² of floor area or part thereof, up to a maximum of 8 persons; and
(c) with a total floor area of not more than 3500 m².
