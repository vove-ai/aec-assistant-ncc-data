# Drainage system

A system that—

(a) conveys water by gravity, mechanical means, or evaporation to a point of discharge or evaporative surface; or
(b) channels water by pipes, overflows, and overland flow paths to a point of discharge.

> The definition for ‘drainage system’ is used only for the purposes of Volume One Section F.
