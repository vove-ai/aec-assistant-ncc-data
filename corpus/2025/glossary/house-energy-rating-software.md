# House energy rating software

For the purposes of—

(a) Volume One, applied to J3D3 or J3D15 — software accredited under the Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (NatHERS); or—
(b) Volume Two—
  (i) applied to H6V2 — software accredited or previously accredited under the Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (NatHERS) and the additional functionality provided in non-regulatory mode; and
  (ii) applied to Specification 42 — software accredited under the Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (NatHERS).

> The Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (NatHERS) refers to the Australian Governments' scheme that facilitates consistent energy ratings from software tools which are used to assess the potential thermal efficiency of dwelling envelopes.
