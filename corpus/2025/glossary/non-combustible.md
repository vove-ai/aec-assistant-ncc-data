# Non-combustible

Applied to—

(a) a material — means not deemed combustible as determined by an Accredited Testing Laboratory in accordance with AS 1530.1 — Combustibility Tests for Materials; or
(b) construction or part of a building — means constructed wholly of materials that are not deemed combustible.

> Until the adoption of the next edition of the NCC determination need not be undertaken by an Accredited Testing Laboratory.
