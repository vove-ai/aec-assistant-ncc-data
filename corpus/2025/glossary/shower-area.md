# Shower area

The area affected by water from a shower, including a shower over a bath and for a shower area that is—

(a) Enclosed – the area enclosed by walls or screens including hinged or sliding doors that contain the spread of water to within that space; or
(b) Unenclosed – the area where, under normal use, water from the shower rose is not contained within the shower area.
