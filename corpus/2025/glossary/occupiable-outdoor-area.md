# Occupiable outdoor area

A space on a roof, balcony or similar part of a building—

(a) that is open to the sky; and
(b) to which access is provided, other than access only for maintenance; and
(c) that is not open space or directly connected with open space.

> For the purposes of this definition, a minor roof covering that is open sided, such as an awning provided at a doorway, does not prevent an area from being considered ‘open to the sky’.
