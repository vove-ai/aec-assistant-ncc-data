# Fire hazard properties

The following properties of a material or assembly that indicate how they behave under specific fire test conditions:

(a) Critical radiant flux and smoke development rate, determined in accordance with AS ISO 9239.1.
(b) Smoke-Developed Index and Spread-of-Flame Index, determined in accordance with AS/NZS 1530.3.
(c) Group number, average specific extinction area and Smoke growth rate index (SMOGRARC), determined in accordance with AS 5637.1.
