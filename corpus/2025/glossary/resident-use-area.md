# Resident use area

Part of a Class 9c building normally used by residents, and—

(a) includes sole-occupancy units, lounges, dining areas, activity rooms and the like; but
(b) excludes offices, storage areas, commercial kitchens, commercial laundries and other spaces not for the use of residents.
