# Fire-protected steel

Loadbearing wall construction comprising hot rolled or cold formed steel members protected with a fire-protective covering that consists of—

(a) not less than 2 layers of 13 mm fire-protective grade plasterboard applied to each side of the wall; and
(b) additional fire-protective covering to that required by (a) where necessary to achieve the FRL required for the wall.
