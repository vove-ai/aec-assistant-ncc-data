# Smoke development rate

The development rate for smoke as determined by an Accredited Testing Laboratory testing flooring materials in accordance with AS ISO 9239.1.

> Until the adoption of the next edition of the NCC determination need not be undertaken by an Accredited Testing Laboratory.
