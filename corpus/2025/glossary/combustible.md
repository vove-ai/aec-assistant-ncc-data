# Combustible

Applied to—

(a) a material — means combustible as determined by an Accredited Testing Laboratory in accordance with AS 1530.1; and
(b) construction or part of a building — means constructed wholly or in part of combustible materials.

> Until the adoption of the next edition of the NCC determination need not be undertaken by an Accredited Testing Laboratory.
