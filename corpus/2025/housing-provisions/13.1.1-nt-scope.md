# 13.1.1 — Scope

This Section sets out the following Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for energy efficiency:

(a) Building fabric (see NT Part 13.2).
(b) External glazing (see NT Part 13.3).
(c) Building sealing (see NT Part 13.4).
(d) Air movement (see NT Part 13.5).
(e) Services (see NT Part 13.7).
