# 4.2.12 — Footing and slab construction

Footing and slab construction, including size and placement of reinforcement, must be in accordance with the relevant provisions of—

(a) 4.2.13 for footings for stumps; and
(b) 4.2.14 for stiffened rafts on Class A, S and M sites; and
(c) 4.2.15 for strip footing systems on Class A, S and M sites; and
(d) 4.2.16 for footing slabs on Class A sites; and
(e) 4.2.17 for footings for single leaf masonry, mixed construction and earth retaining walls; and
(f) 4.2.18 for footings for fireplaces on Class A and S sites; and
(g) 4.2.19 for shrinkage control; and
(h) 4.2.20 for concentrated loads; and
(i) 4.2.21 for minimum edge beam dimensions; and
(j) 4.2.22 for recessed areas of slabs.
