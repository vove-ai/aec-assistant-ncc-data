# 4.2.18 — Footings for fireplaces on Class A and S sites

**(1)** Fireplaces on Class A and S sites must be supported on a pad footing—

(a) 150 mm thick for single storey (one trafficable floor and a wall height not more than 4.2 m) construction; and
(b) 200 mm thick for 2 storey (two trafficable floors and a wall height not more than 8 m) construction; and
(c) reinforced top and bottom with SL72 mesh; and
(d) extending 300 mm past the edges of the masonry except for any edge flush with the outer wall.

**(2)** The pad footing must form an integral part of the slab.
