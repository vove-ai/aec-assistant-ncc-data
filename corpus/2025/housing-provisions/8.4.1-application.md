# 8.4.1 — Application

**(1)** Part 8.4 applies subject to the limitations set out in H1D8(1).

**(2)** Part 8.4 need not be complied with if H1D8(3)(a) is complied with.

**NSW variation (REPLACE) — Application**

**(2)** Part 8.4 need not be complied with if NSW H1D8(3)(a) is complied with.

**(3)** The thickness and type of glazing installed in areas of a building that have a high potential for human impact (an area of a building frequented by the occupants during everyday activities in which a person could fall into or against the glazed panel) must comply as follows:

(a) Doors — in accordance with 8.4.2.
(b) Door side panels — in accordance with 8.4.3.
(c) Full height glass panels — in accordance with 8.4.4.
(d) Glazed panels, other than doors or side panels, on the perimeter of rooms — in accordance with 8.4.5.
(e) Bathrooms, ensuite and spa room glazing — in accordance with 8.4.6.
(f) Visibility of glazing — in accordance with 8.4.7.
(g) Identification of safety glass — in accordance with 8.4.8.
