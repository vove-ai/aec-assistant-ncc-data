# 11.2.1 — Explanation of terms

**(1)** Figure 11.2.1 depicts stairway members and associated terminology used to describe them in the ABCB Housing Provisions.

**(2)** Some items such as barriers and handrails have been omitted for clarity.

![Figure 11.2.1: Stairway terms](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2025/housing/image-11-2-1-stairway-terms.svg)

> **Alpine areas**
>
> The requirements of this Part are to be read in conjunction with Part 12.2 where a building is located in an alpine area and contains an external stairway or ramp.

> **Room heights**
>
> Part 10.3 contains the required height for a ceiling above a stairway, ramp or landing, measured vertically above the nosing line of stairway treads or the floor surface of a ramp or landing.
