# WA Introduction

This Appendix contains variations and additions to the Building Code of Australia (BCA) which are necessary for its application in Western Australia. For the ABCB Housing Provisions Standard these apply to:

- the fire separation of roofed outdoor areas,
- the design and installation of eaves gutters,
- windows installed in masonry walls.
- building fabric and external glazing for climate zone 6; and
- installation of floors of upper storeys in climate zones 4 and 5.

Note: The Building Regulations 2012 in Western Australia make other modifications to the BCA. These modifications may change during a BCA cycle, and include:

- Regulation 15B - modifications to AS 1926.1-2012, and
- Regulation 15C - the dis-application of the livable (accessible) housing provisions and energy efficiency heating and cooling load limits making them voluntary.

Refer to the latest Building Regulations 2012 available from www.legislation.wa.gov.au for other modifications.
