# 4.2.7 — Stepped footings

Stepped strip footings must—

(a) have a base that is horizontal or be sloped at not more than 1:10; or
(b) be stepped in accordance with one of the methods shown in Figure 4.2.7.

![Figure 4.2.7: Stepped strip footings](https://cdn.aecassistant.com.au/images/ncc/2025/housing/image-4-2-7-stepped-strip-footings.svg)

> All dimensions in millimetres.
