# 5.5.4 — Isolated piers supporting sheet roofs

Isolated piers supporting sheet roofs must have—

(a) a built-in 30 x 0.8 mm galvanised steel strap fixed to the roof structure extending the full height of the pier which is looped and fixed around a 4.6 grade 16 mm diameter galvanised steel rod cast into the footing when poured; or
(b) a 4.6 grade M16 galvanised steel rod cast into the footing, threaded at the top and extending the full height of the pier to connect to the roof structure.
