# 13.4.3 — Roof lights

**(1)** A roof light must be sealed, or capable of being sealed, when serving a conditioned space.

**(2)** A roof light required by (1) must be constructed with—

(a) an imperforate ceiling diffuser or the like installed at the ceiling or internal lining level; or
(b) a weatherproof seal if it is a roof window; or
(c) a shutter system readily operated either manually, mechanically or electronically by the occupant.
