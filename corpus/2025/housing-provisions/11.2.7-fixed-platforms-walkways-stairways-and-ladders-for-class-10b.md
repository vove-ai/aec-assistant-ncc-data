# 11.2.7 — Fixed platforms, walkways, stairways and ladders for Class 10b structures

A fixed platform, walkway, stairway or ladder and associated landings serving a Class 10b structure must comply with—

(a) AS 1657; or
(b) for—
  (i) stairways — 11.2.2 and 11.2.4; and
  (ii) landings — 11.2.4 and 11.2.5.
