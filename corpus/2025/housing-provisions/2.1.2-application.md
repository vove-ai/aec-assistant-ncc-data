# 2.1.2 — Application

The application of Section 2 is subject to the following:

(a) The Governing Requirements of NCC Volume Two.
(b) The State and Territory variations, additions and deletions contained in the Schedules to the ABCB Housing Provisions and NCC Volume Two.

> In NCC 2019, the content of Section 2 of the ABCB Housing Provisions (other than content added in NCC 2022 or later) was contained in Part 3.0 of NCC Volume Two.
