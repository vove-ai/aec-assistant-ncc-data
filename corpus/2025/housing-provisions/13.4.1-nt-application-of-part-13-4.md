# 13.4.1 — Application of Part 13.4

This Part applies to—

(a) a Class 1 building; and
(b) a Class 10a building with a conditioned space.

> This Part does not apply to:
>
> (a) A building where the only means of air-conditioning is by using an evaporative cooler.
> (b) A permanent building ventilation opening that is necessary for the safe operation of a gas appliance.
> (c) A Class 10a building used for the accommodation of vehicles.
