# 9.1.1 — Scope

**(1)** This Section sets out the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for—

(a) fire separation of external walls (see Part 9.2); and
(b) fire protection of separating walls (see Part 9.3); and
(c) fire separation of garage top dwellings (see Part 9.4); and
(d) smoke alarms and evacuation lighting (see Part 9.5).

**NSW variation (REPLACE) — Scope**

**(1)** This Section sets out the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for—

(a) fire separation of external walls (see Part 9.2); and
(b) fire protection of separating walls (see Part 9.3); and
(c) fire protection of garage top dwellings (see NSW Part 9.4); and
(d) smoke alarms and evacuation lighting (see Part 9.5).

**(2)** For other fire safety provisions not included in this Section, refer to NCC Volume Two H3D2(1) and (2): Fire hazard properties.
