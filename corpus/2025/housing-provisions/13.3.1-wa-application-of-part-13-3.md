# 13.3.1 — Application of Part 13.3

**(1)** This Part applies to—

(a) a Class 1 building; and
(b) a Class 10a building with a conditioned space.

**(2)** Part 13.3 must be applied as directed in H6D2(1)(b).

**(3)** For the purpose of Part 13.3, references to climate zone 6 in Figure 2 and Table 3 under the definition of climate zone in the Schedule 1 may be considered as climate zone 5.

> The option to substitute climate zone 5 for climate zone 6 only applies to Parts 13.2 and 13.3 of the ABCB Housing Provisions.
>
> Provisions relating to the selection and installation of services and condensation management in buildings are unaffected by this variation.
