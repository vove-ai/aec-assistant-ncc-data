# S17C13 — Sprinkler systems in lift installations

**(1)** Where sprinklers are installed in a space housing lift electrical and control equipment, including machine rooms, secondary floors and sheave rooms, sprinklers in these spaces must—

(a) have heads protected from accidental damage by way of a guard that will not impair the performance of the head; and
(b) be capable of being isolated and drained, either separately or collectively, without isolating any other sprinklers within the building.

**(2)** Valves provided to control sprinklers referred to in (1) must be installed in accordance with S17C10(2).
