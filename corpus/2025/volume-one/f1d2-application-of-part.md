# F1D2 — Application of Part

**(1)** F1D4 to F1D7 and F1D10 do not apply to a roof with a covering complying with F1D12(a) to (d).

**(2)** F1D3 to F1D7 and F1D10 do not apply to—

(a) timber decking or other perforated flooring to a balcony, podium or similar horizontal surface part of a building; or
(b) a balcony, podium or similar horizontal surface part of a building which is located directly above ground.

**(3)** F1D3 to F1D15 do not apply to horizontal or near horizontal surfaces created by window hoods, spandrels, tops of parapets and other features.

**SA variation (REPLACE) — Application of Part**

**(3)** F1D3 to F1D8, SA F1D9 and F1D10 to F1D15 do not apply to horizontal or near horizontal surfaces created by window hoods, spandrels, tops of parapets and other features.

**(4)** For the purpose of this Part, reference to a horizontal element includes reference to a near horizontal element.
