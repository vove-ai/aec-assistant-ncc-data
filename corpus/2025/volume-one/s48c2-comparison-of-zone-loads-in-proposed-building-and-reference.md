# S48C2 — Comparison of zone loads in proposed building and reference building

**(1)** The zone load is the sensible heating or cooling effect required to maintain the temperature in each zone within the range specified in S34C3(8).

**(2)** The loads for each zone in the proposed building and the reference building must be calculated in accordance with the modelling parameters of Specification 34.

**(3)** A zone of the proposed building is assessable under J1V3(1)(b)(iii) to (v) if it has—

(a) a 98th percentile heating load of at least 10 W/m2: or
(b) a 98th percentile cooling load of at least 40 W/m2.
