# B1D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements B1P1 to B1P3 are satisfied by complying with B1D2 to B1D6.

**NSW variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements NSW B1P1, B1P2, B1P3 and NSW B1P4 are satisfied by complying with B1D2 to B1D6.

**(2)** Where a Performance Solution is proposed, the relevant Performance Requirements must be determined in accordance with A2G2(3) and A2G4(3) as applicable.
