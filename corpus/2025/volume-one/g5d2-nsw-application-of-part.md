# G5D2 — Application of Part

The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply in a designated bushfire prone area to—

(a) a Class 2 or 3 building; or
(b) a Class 4 part of a building; or
(c) a Class 9 building that is a special fire protection purpose —
  (i) located in an area subject to radiant heat levels of not greater than 10kW/m2 (calculated at 1200k), determined in accordance with Planning for Bush Fire Protection; or
  (ii) where the development consent or a bushfire safety authority issued under Section 100B of the Rural Fires Act 1997 states that the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions apply; or
(d) a Class 10a building or deck immediately adjacent or connected to a building or part of a type in (a), (b) or (c).

> (1) If a building of a type listed in (c) or (d) where associated with a building listed in (c) is subject to a radiant heat levels of greater than 10kW/m2 (calculated at 1200K), the building would need to comply with Performance Requirement NSW G5P2 by means of a Performance Solution. Unless the development consent or a bushfire safety authority issued under section 100B of the Rural Fires Act 1997 has stated that the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this part can apply.
> (2) Other than the circumstances listed in (1) there are no Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions for buildings of the types listed in (c) or (d) where associated with a building listed in (c).
