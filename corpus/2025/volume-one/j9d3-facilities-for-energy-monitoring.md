# J9D3 — Facilities for energy monitoring

**(1)** A building or sole-occupancy unit with a floor area of more than 500 m2 must have energy meters configured to record the time-of-use consumption of gas and electricity.

**(2)** A building with a floor area of more than 2 500 m2 must have energy meters configured to enable individual time-of-use energy data recording, in accordance with (3), of—

(a) air-conditioning plant including, where appropriate, heating plant, cooling plant and air handling fans; and
(b) artificial lighting; and
(c) appliance power; and
(d) central hot water supply; and
(e) internal transport devices including lifts, escalators and moving walkways where there is more than one serving the building; and
(f) on-site renewable energy equipment; and
(g) on-site electric vehicle charging equipment; and
(h) on-site battery systems; and
(i) other ancillary plant.

**(3)** Energy meters required by (2) must be interlinked by a communication system that collates the time-of-use energy data to a single interface monitoring system where it can be stored, analysed and reviewed.

**(4)** The provisions of (2) do not apply to energy meters serving individual sole-occupancy units with a floor area of less than 2 500 m2.

**(5)** Where a building has an Environmental Management Information System facility it must record, in addition to J9D3(2)(a) to (i), on a 5 minute time interval basis—

(a) the energy use of any piece of equipment predicted to consume more than 5% of total building annual energy use; and
(b) entering and leaving temperatures and energy use for each chiller, heat pump, electric heater or hot water heater used in air-conditioning; and
(c) the temperature of all internal air-conditioning zones; and
(d) the water flow and return temperature of chilled and heated water loops; and
(e) the external temperature and humidity local to the building; and
(f) measurement of internal carbon dioxide and car park pollutant levels, where these are used for controlling fan speed or outside air quantities; and
(g) total instantaneous building electricity demand, recorded as an average over every 5 minutes of building operation; and
(h) the start/stop command value and speed of air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation fans and pumps and associated system pressure measurement and set point; and
(i) the damper command for economy cycle and ventilation dampers; and
(j) the damper or valve command for energy reclaim equipment.

**(6)** The Environmental Management Information System in (5) must—

(a) allow demand management control of the building’s—
  (i) renewable energy system, including any battery energy storage system installed; and
  (ii) lighting system; and
  (iii) air-conditioning and ventilation system; and
  (iv) electric vehicle charging equipment; and
(b) include an operational interface that enables remote users to visualise system data and operational status; and
(c) allow for the visualisation of data in a graphical format, including the ability to compare individual data streams integrated over time periods and in time series; and
(d) be able to provide bulk downloads of at least 2 years of historic time series data to users; and
(e) allow scheduling, supervisory level setpoint override and modulation set-back of the air-conditioning and ventilation system; and
(f) incorporate monitoring and verification functionality with the capability to—
  (i) provide monitoring and verification of a change in energy use in accordance with the International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol Options B and C; and
  (ii) automatically determine an energy performance baseline from historical building data and weather data; and
  (iii) compare building performance against the baseline, both numerically and graphically; and
  (iv) provide forecasts of the building’s renewable energy production.

**(7)** The requirements of (5) and (6) do not apply to services that consume less than 5% of the building’s total annual energy consumption.

> An Environmental Management Information System that complies with J9D3(5) and (6) and records data as required by J9D3(2)(a) to (i) satisfies the requirement for ‘energy meters’ in J9D3(2).
