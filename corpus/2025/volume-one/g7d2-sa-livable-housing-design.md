# G7D2 — Livable housing design

**(1)** Each sole-occupancy unit in a Class 2 building must comply with the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design, except for Part 1.

**(2)** The sanitary compartment that is subject to Clause 4.1 of the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design may be located on either:

(a) the ground or entry level; or
(b) the lowest level containing a habitable room within the sole-occupancy unit of a Class 2 building.

> Part 1 of the [ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design](https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/system/files/ncc/ncc2025-livable-housing-design.pdf) requires a step-free access path from a parking space or property boundary, to the dwelling entry door. For Class 2 buildings, this requirement is already addressed by Part D4 and the Disability (Access to Premises—Buildings) Standards 2010. Therefore, it is not necessary to apply Part 1 of the ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design to a Class 2 building.
