# F1D7 — External waterproofing membranes

**(1)** A roof, balcony, podium or similar horizontal surface part of a building must be provided with a waterproofing membrane —

(a) consisting of materials complying with AS 4654.1; and
(b) designed and installed in accordance with AS 4654.2.

**(2)** Where a membrane required by (1) is applied to a concrete roof, balcony, podium or similar horizontal surface, the membrane must be installed directly on a structural substrate complying with F1D5.
