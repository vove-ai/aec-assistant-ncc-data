# B1D6 — Construction of buildings in flood hazard areas

This clause has deliberately been left blank.

Building work in designated flood hazard areas is regulated by the Building Act 1975 (QLD), and the Queensland Development Code MP 3.5 - Construction of buildings in flood hazard areas.
