# I4D8 — Conventional stages: large stages

A stage which is more than 150 m2 in area—

(a) must have installed directly above the stage a suitable sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17; and
(b) must have the proscenium opening protected by a safety curtain that complies with NSW I4D15 and NSW I4D16; and
(c) must have a line of open drenchers or open sprinklers provided above the proscenium opening on the stage side and in such a position as to be able to discharge over the inside face of the safety curtain; and
(d) must have 2 or more means of egress from the stage and backstage area provided otherwise than through the proscenium wall.
