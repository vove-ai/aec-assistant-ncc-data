# F1D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements F1P1 is satisfied by complying with F1D2 to F1D15.

**SA variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements F1P1 and SA F1P2 are satisfied by complying with F1D2 to F1D8, SA F1D9 and F1D10 to F1D15.

**(2)** Where a Performance Solution is proposed, the relevant Performance Requirements must be determined in accordance with A2G2(3) and A2G4(3) as applicable.
