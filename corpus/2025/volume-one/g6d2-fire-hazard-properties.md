# G6D2 — Fire hazard properties

**(1)** Subject to (2), a lining, material or assembly in an occupiable outdoor area must comply with C2D11 as for an internal element.

**(2)** The following fire hazard properties of a lining, material or assembly in an occupiable outdoor area are not required to comply with C2D11:

(a) Average specific extinction area.
(b) Smoke-Developed Index.
(c) Smoke development rate.
(d) Smoke growth rate index (SMOGRARC).
