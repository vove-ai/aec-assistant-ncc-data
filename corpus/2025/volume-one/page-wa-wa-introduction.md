# WA Introduction

This Appendix contains variations and additions to the Building Code of Australia (BCA) which are necessary for its application in Western Australia. For Volume One these apply to:

- the design and installation of eaves gutters,
- swimming pool barriers; and
- building related requirements from the Health (Public Buildings) Regulations 1992 which are consolidated into the Western Australian Schedule of the BCA.

Note: The Building Regulations 2012 in Western Australia, make other modifications to the BCA. These modifications may change during a BCA cycle, and include:

- Regulation 15B - modifications to AS 1926.1-2012,
- Regulation 15C - the dis-application of the energy efficiency heating and cooling load limits making them voluntary; and
- Regulation 15D - a transitional period making NCC Part G5 bushfire construction requirements voluntary for Class 9b primary and secondary schools and early childhood centres until 30 April 2028.

Refer to the latest Building Regulations 2012 available from [www.legislation.wa.gov.au](https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/) for other modifications.
