# D2D24 — Doorways to a children’s room

In an early childhood centre, a children’s room must—

(a) have a doorway providing direct access to or form—
  (i) an outdoor play area; or
  (ii) a passage leading to an outdoor area, open space or other safe egress point; or
  (iii) a fire-isolated exit; and
(b) where the children’s room accommodates more than 21 children, at least two doorways complying with (a) that are separated as far as practicable.
