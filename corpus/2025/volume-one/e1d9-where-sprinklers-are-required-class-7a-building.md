# E1D9 — Where sprinklers are required: Class 7a building

In a Class 7a building, other than an open-deck carpark which is a separate building, sprinklers are required in fire compartments where—

(a) more than 40 vehicles are accommodated; or
(b) a carstacker is installed.

> A carstacker is a machine that stores 2 or more cars in a vertical arrangement such that one or more cars may not be accessible from floor level.
