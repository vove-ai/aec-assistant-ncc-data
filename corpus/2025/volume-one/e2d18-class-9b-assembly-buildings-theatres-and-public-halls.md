# E2D18 — Class 9b – assembly buildings: theatres and public halls

**(1)** This clause applies to a Class 9b assembly building where the building or part of the building is used as a theatre or public hall which—

(a) is a school assembly, church or community hall, and has a stage and any backstage area with a total floor area of more than 300 m2; or
(b) is not a school assembly, church or community hall, and has a stage and any backstage area with a total floor area of more than 200 m2; or
(c) has a stage with an associated rigging loft.

**(2)** A building or part of a building referred to in (1) must be provided with—

(a) an automatic smoke exhaust system complying with Specification 21; or
(b) if the building is single storey, automatic smoke-and-heat vents complying with Specification 22.
