# J4D2 — Application of Part

The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply to building elements forming the envelope of a Class 3 to 9 building other than J4D3(5), J4D4, J4D5, J4D6 and J4D7 which do not apply to a Class 4 part of a building.
