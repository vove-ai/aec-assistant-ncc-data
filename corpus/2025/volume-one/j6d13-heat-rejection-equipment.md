# J6D13 — Heat rejection equipment

**(1)** The motor rated power of a fan in a cooling tower, closed circuit cooler or evaporative condenser must not exceed the allowances in Table J6D13.

**(2)** The fan in an air-cooled condenser must have a motor rated power of not more than 42 W for each kW of heat rejected from the refrigerant when determined in accordance with AHRI 460.

**(3)** A fan with a three-phase motor in heat rejection equipment must be capable of variable speed operation.

**(4)** The requirements of (2) and (3) do not apply to—

(a) a fan in a refrigerant chiller of an air-conditioning system that complies with J6D11; and
(b) fans in unitary air-conditioning equipment, such as a packaged air-conditioners, a split system or a variable refrigerant flow unit that complies with J6D12; and
(c) a fan in a 4-pipe chiller or air source heat pump water heater that complies with J6D10 (8).

### Table J6D13 — Maximum fan motor power – Cooling towers, closed circuit coolers and evaporative condensers

| Type | Cooling tower maximum fan motor input power (W/kW) | Closed circuit cooler maximum fan motor input power (W/kW) | Evaporative condenser maximum fan motor input power (W/kW) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Induced draft | 10.4 | 16.9 | 11.0 |
| Forced draft | 19.5 | Note | 11.0 |

> A closed circuit, forced draft cooling tower must not be used.
