# Energy efficiency

This Part has deliberately been left blank.

For a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2009 (without NT variation). For Class 3 and Class 5-9 buildings, Section J of NCC 2025 does not apply and is replaced with Section J of NCC 2019 (without NT variation).
