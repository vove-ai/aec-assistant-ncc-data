# G1P6 — Cleaning of windows

A building must provide for a safe manner for the cleaning of windows.

> NSW G1P6 only applies where any windows are located 3 or more storeys above ground level.

> Any measures provided to facilitate the safe cleaning of windows should ensure that the persons undertaking the activity of cleaning the windows and the persons responsible for the safety of persons undertaking the activity of cleaning the windows are able to satisfy their obligations under the Work Health and Safety Act 2011, and Regulations made under that Act.
