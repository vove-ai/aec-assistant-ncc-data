# E1P2 — Fire extinguishers

Fire extinguishers must be installed to the degree necessary to allow occupants to undertake initial attack on a fire appropriate to—

(a) the function or use of the building; and
(b) any other fire safety systems installed in the building; and
(c) the fire hazard.
