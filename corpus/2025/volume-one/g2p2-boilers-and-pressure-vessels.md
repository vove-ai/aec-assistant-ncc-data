# G2P2 — Boilers and pressure vessels

When located in a building, boilers and pressure vessels must be installed to avoid, during reasonably foreseeable conditions, the likelihood of—

(a) leakage from the vessel which could cause damage to the building; and
(b) rupture or other mechanical damage of the vessel which could cause damage to the building or injury to occupants.
