# I4D8 — Smoke venting

**(1)** In a bulk grain storage facility, suitable natural smoke venting by fixed vents must be provided at the top of elevator towers and overhead galleries in a cell type silo.

**(2)** Smoke venting is not required in a storage shed of a bulk grain storage facility.
