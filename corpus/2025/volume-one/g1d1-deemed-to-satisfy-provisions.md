# G1D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements G1P1 to G1P5 are satisfied by complying with G1D2 to G1D5.

**SA variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements G1P1, SA G1P2, G1P3 to G1P5 are satisfied by complying with G1D2 to G1D5.

**NSW variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements G1P1 to NSW G1P6 are satisfied by complying with NSW G1D2 to NSW G1D6.

**TAS variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(2)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements G1P2 to G1P5 and TAS G1P6 are satisfied by complying with TAS G1D2 to G1D4.

**(2)** Where a Performance Solution is proposed, the relevant Performance Requirements must be determined in accordance with A2G2(3) and A2G4(3) as applicable.
