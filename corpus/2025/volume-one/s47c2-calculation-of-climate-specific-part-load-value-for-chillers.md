# S47C2 — Calculation of climate specific part load value for chillers

**(1)** The climate specific part load value for a group of one or more chillers must be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

CSPLV=α_(100)EER_(100)+α_(75)EER_(75)+α_(50)EER_(50)+α_(25)EER_(25)

where—

(a) CSPLV= climate specific part load value; and
(a) EER_n = the weighted average energy efficiency ratio of the chillers that operate to provide n% of the design cooling load under the staging strategy to be deployed, determined in accordance with (2) and (3); and
(b) α_n = load weighting factors specified in—
  (i) Table S47C2a for chillers serving a Class 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9b building or a Class 9a building other than a ward area; or
  (ii) Table S47C2b for chillers serving a Class 3 or 9c building or a Class 9a ward area.

**(2)** EER_n is determined as the weighted average energy efficiency ratio of the chillers operating to meet n% of the design cooling load under the staging strategy to be deployed, allowing for the part load efficiencies at—

(a) a chilled water leaving temperature of 7°C; and
(b) a chilled water flow rate that corresponds to a 6°C difference between entering and leaving temperatures at the rated cooling capacity, when determined in accordance with AHRI 551/591; and
(c) for water-cooled chillers—
  (i) a condenser water entering temperature that is determined by adding the design approach temperature to the outside wet bulb temperature listed in—
    (A) Table S47C2c for chillers serving a Class 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9b building or a Class 9a building other than a ward area; or
    (B) Table S47C2d for chillers serving a Class 3 or 9c building or a Class 9a ward area; and
  (ii) a condenser water flow rate that corresponds to a 6°C difference between entering and leaving temperature at the rated cooling capacity, when determined in accordance with AHRI 551/591; and
(d) for air-cooled chillers, the outside dry bulb temperature listed in—
  (i) Table S47C2e for chillers serving a Class 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9b building or a Class 9a building other than a ward area; or
  (ii) Table S47C2f for a Class 3 or 9c building or a Class 9a ward area.

**(3)** The EER_n values in (2) must be determined using test measurements and calculations performed in accordance with the procedures of AHRI 551/591.
