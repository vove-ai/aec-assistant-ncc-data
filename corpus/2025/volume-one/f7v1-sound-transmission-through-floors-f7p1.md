# F7V1 — Sound transmission through floors [F7P1]

Compliance with F7P1 to avoid the transmission of airborne and impact generated sound through floors is verified when it is measured in-situ that the separating floor has—

(a) airborne: a weighted standardised level difference with spectrum adaptation term (DnT,w + Ctr) not less than 45 when determined under AS ISO 717.1; and
(b) impact: a weighted standardised impact sound pressure level (LnT,w) not more than 62 when determined under AS ISO 717.2.
