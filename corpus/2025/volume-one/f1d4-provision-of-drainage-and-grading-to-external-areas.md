# F1D4 — Provision of drainage and grading to external areas

A concrete roof, balcony, podium or similar part of a building must have—

(a) a drainage system complying with F1D3 that incorporates sufficient overflow provision: and
(b) its structural substrate graded with a minimum fall of 1:80 in the plane of the substrate to suit the drainage system required by (a); and
(c) a minimum 70 mm step down from the surface level of the internal floor structural substrate to the adjacent surface level of the external structural substrate, where applicable; and
(d) a monolithic hob that is integral with the structural substrate, at least 70 mm high, installed at the outer perimeter, except where the perimeter of the roof, balcony, podium or similar part of the building drains directly to a gutter.

> (1) The invert level of overflow provision referred to in F1D4(a) must be installed not less than 20 mm below any adjacent internal floor structural substrate.
> (2) For the purposes of Section F, the plane of the floor or substrate is taken to mean the predominant fall of the surface in question, typically perpendicular to bounding walls and is not a reference to the diagonal intersection between planes.
> (3) For the purposes of this Part, a tile bed, screed, topping or similar component is not considered a structural substrate except within planter boxes where it can be used to achieve the minimum fall of 1:80.
> (4) F1D4(d) does not prevent penetration of a hob for the provision of overflow.
> (5) The minimum 70 mm step down reference in F1D4(c) is not intended to refer to the minimum waterproofing termination heigh specified in AS 4654.2. Where AS 4654.2 requires a termination heigh greater than 70 mm, the waterproof membrane termination can be extended by suitable means above the 70 mm concrete set down.

> Note that F1D7(1)(b), by reference to AS 4654.2, may require in some circumstances a step down greater than 70 mm.
>
> Similarly, other provisions may also specify gradient requirements. For example, D4D2 may prevent gradients steeper than 1:40.
