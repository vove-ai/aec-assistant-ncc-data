# B1D2 — Resistance to actions

The resistance of a building or structure must be greater than the most critical action effect resulting from different combinations of actions, where—

(a) the most critical action effect on a building or structure is determined in accordance with B1D3 and the general design procedures contained in AS/NZS 1170.0; and
(b) design for the actions in (a) accommodates expected 10-year deflection for structural substrates in Part F1; and
(c) the resistance of a building or structure is determined in accordance with B1D4.
