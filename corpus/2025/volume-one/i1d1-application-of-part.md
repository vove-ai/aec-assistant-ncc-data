# I1D1 — Application of Part

**(1)** The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply to every enclosed Class 9b building or part of a building which—

(a) is a school assembly, church or community hall with a stage and any backstage area with a total floor area of more than 300 m2; or
(b) otherwise, has a stage and any backstage area with a total floor area of more than 200 m2; or
(c) has a stage with an associated rigging loft.

**(2)** Notwithstanding (1)—

(a) I1D4 applies to every open or enclosed Class 9b building; and
(b) I1D7 applies to every enclosed Class 9b building.

> Part I1 contains Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions additional to those contained in Sections C, D and E for Class 9b buildings.
