# I4D10 — Ventilation

In a bulk grain storage facility, a habitable room, office, shop, factory, workroom, sanitary compartment, bathroom, shower room, laundry and any other room occupied by a person for any purpose must have a ventilation system that provides one air change every six hours using openings that have a total area of the lesser of—

(a) 35 m2; or
(b) one percent of the total floor area.
