# Water management

This Part is intended to minimise the risk of water leaking into or accumulating within a building and causing unhealthy conditions or damaging building elements. It is also intended to prevent water redirected away from the building damaging nearby properties.

> **NSW Part F1**
>
> For NSW, Parts F1 and F3 of NCC 2022 Amendment 2 Volume One may apply instead of Part F1 of NCC 2025 Volume One.

> **NSW Part F1**
>
> Note to NSW Part F1 applies to building work undertaken on an existing part of an existing building, where the existing building was subject to compliance with NCC 2022 Amendment 2 Volume One, an earlier edition of NCC Volume One, the Building Code of Australia or its predecessors.

> **NSW Part F1**
>
> Notes and Applications to NSW Part F1 provides the option for building work to existing parts of an existing building to comply with Parts F1 and F3 of NCC 2022 Amendment 2 or Part F1 of NCC 2025. New buildings and new extensions/additions to an existing building must comply with Part F1 of NCC 2025.
