# I4D2 — Type of construction required

The external walls of a bulk grain storage facility need not be of fire-resisting construction in accordance with Table C2D2 if—

(a) the external walls are 3 m or more from an allotment boundary and more than 6 m from any other building on the same allotment, other than a Class 10 building; and
(b) a fire separation space of not less than 2 m is provided between cell type silos; and
(c) the external walls are of—
  (i) non-combustible construction; or
  (ii) Type C construction.
