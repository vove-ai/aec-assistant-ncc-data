# Elemental provisions for a Class 4 part of a building

This Part has deliberately been left blank.

This Part is deleted from the BCA in Tasmania. In Tasmania, for a Class 2 building and Class 4 part of a building, Section J is replaced with Section J of BCA 2019 Amendment 1.
