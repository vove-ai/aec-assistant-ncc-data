# I5D14 — Electrical services

Electrical services connected to the local supply authority’s mains, to a generating plant or to a battery supply must comply with—

(a) the requirements of the local supply authority; and
(b) AS 3002; and
(c) where applicable, AS/NZS 3000; and
(d) NSW I4D51(a) and NSW I4D53(a).
