# I4D1 — Application of Part

**(1)** The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply to bulk grain storage facilities in South Australia.

**(2)** The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part take precedence where there is a difference to the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of Sections C, D, E and F.

> SA Part I4 contains Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions additional to those contained in Sections C, D, E and F for bulk grain storage facilities.
