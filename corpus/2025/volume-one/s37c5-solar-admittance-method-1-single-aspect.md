# S37C5 — Solar admittance — Method 1 (Single Aspect)

**(1)** The solar admittance of a wall-glazing construction must be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

SA=(A_(W1)×S_(W1)×SHGC_(W1))/(A_(WALL))+(A_(W2)×S_(W2)×SHGC_(W2))/(A_(WALL))+...

**(2)** In the formula at (1)—

(a) SA = the wall-glazing construction solar admittance; and
(b) A_(W1),A_(W2),... = the area of each glazing element; and
(c) S_(W1),S_(W2,...)= the shading multiplier for each glazing element in accordance with S37C7; and
(d) SHGC_(W1,W2,...)= the total system SHGC of each glazing element; and
(e) A_(WALL) = the total wall-glazing construction area.
