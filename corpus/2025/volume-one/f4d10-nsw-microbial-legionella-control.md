# F4D10 — Microbial (legionella) control

This clause has deliberately been left blank.

F4D10 does not apply in NSW as the installation of hot water, warm water and cooling water systems (and their operation and maintenance) is regulated in the Public Health Regulation 2022, under the Public Health Act 2010.
