# J5D7 — Construction of roofs, ceilings, walls and floors

**(1)** Ceilings, walls, floors and any opening such as a window frame, door frame, roof light frame, ventilation opening frame or the like must be constructed to minimise air leakage.

**(2)** Construction required by (1) must be—

(a) enclosed by internal lining systems that are close fitting at ceiling, wall and floor junctions; or
(b) sealed at junctions and penetrations with—
  (i) close fitting architrave, skirting or cornice; or
  (ii) expanding foam, rubber compressible strip, caulking or the like.

**(3)** The requirements of (1) do not apply to openings, grilles, louvres or the like required for smoke hazard management.
