# F6D6 — Ventilation of rooms

A habitable room, office, shop, factory, workroom, sanitary compartment, bathroom, shower room, laundry and any other room or space occupied by a person for any purpose must have—

(a) natural ventilation complying with—
  (i) F6D7; or
  (ii) AS 1668.4; or
(b) a mechanical ventilation or air-conditioning system complying with AS 1668.2.

> The reference to AS/NZS 3666.1 is deleted from the BCA in NSW, as the need to comply with this standard is regulated in the Public Health Regulation 2022, under the Public Health Act 2010.
