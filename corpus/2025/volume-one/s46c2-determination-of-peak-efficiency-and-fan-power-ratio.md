# S46C2 — Determination of peak efficiency and fan power ratio

**(1)** The efficiency of a selected fan is the product of its impeller efficiency (including any casing), motor efficiency and the integrated motor control equipment multiplier in (2).

**(2)** The value of the integrated motor control equipment multiplier—

(a) where the fan motor is not capable of variable speed operation or has an external variable speed control device, is 1; or
(b) where the fan motor has integrated variable speed control capability and the motor input power at peak efficiency point is less than 5 kW, −0.03×In(P)+1.088, where—
  (i) P = the motor input power of the fan (kW) at peak efficiency point; and
  (ii) In = natural logarithm; or
(c) where the fan motor has integrated variable speed control capability and the motor input power at peak efficiency point is greater than or equal to 5 KW, is 1.04.

**(3)** The fan power ratio of an individual fan must be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

FPR_(individualfan)=(η_(rf))/(η_(dp))

where—

(a) FPR_(individualfan) = the fan power ratio of the fan; and
(b) η_(rf) = the efficiency calculated using the equation at J6D5(2) or J6D5(3) as applicable, with electrical input power P_(dp), as determined at (5); and
(c) η_(dp) = the static efficiency of the selected fan at the duty point for installation type A or C, or the total efficiency at the duty point for installation type B or D.

**(4)** The average fan power ratio of multiple fans must be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

FPR_(average)=(∑_(applicablefans)P_(dp))/(∑_(applicablefans)P_(rf))

where—

(a) FPR_(average) = the average fan power ratio of multiple fans; and
(b) P_(dp) = the electrical input power of each selected fan at duty point, calculated in accordance with (5); and
(c) P_(rf) = the electrical input power of each reference fan at duty point, calculated in accordance with (6).

**(5)** P_(dp) must be calculated in accordance with the following:

P_(dp)=(p_(dp)Q_(dp))/(η_(dp))

where—

(a) p_(dp) = the static pressure (Pa) at the duty point for installation type A or C or the total pressure (Pa) at the duty point for installation type B or D; and
(b) Q_(dp) = the flow (m3/s) at the duty point; and
(c) η_(dp) = the static efficiency at the duty point for installation type A or C or the total efficiency at the duty point for installation type B or D.

**(6)** P_(rf) must be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

P_(rf)=(p_(dp)Q_(dp))/(η_(rf))

where—

(a) p_(dp) = the static pressure (Pa) at the duty point for installation type A or C or total pressure (Pa) at the duty point for installation type B or D; and
(b) Q_(dp) = the flow (m3/s) at the duty point; and
(c) η_(rf) = the efficiency calculated using the equation at J6D5(2) or (3) as applicable with electrical input power P_(dp).

> Duty point refers to the operating point of the fan when providing the maximum system design flow at the system design pressure. Where the fan is a fixed speed fan, the full speed efficiency and the pressure where the fan curve intersects the system curve may be used.
