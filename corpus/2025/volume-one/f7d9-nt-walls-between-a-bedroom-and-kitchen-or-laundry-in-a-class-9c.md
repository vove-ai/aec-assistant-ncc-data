# F7D9 — Walls between a bedroom and kitchen or laundry in a Class 9c building

In addition to NT F7D5, a wall separating a sole-occupancy unit in a Class 9c building from a kitchen or laundry must—

(a) for other than masonry, be two or more separate leaves without rigid mechanical connection except at their periphery; or
(b) be identical with a prototype that is no less resistant to the transmission of impact sound when tested in accordance with NT Specification 29 than a wall listed in NT S28C3.
