# S16C3 — Zero depth entry

A zero depth entry must have—

(a) a slip-resistant surface; and
(b) a maximum gradient of 1:14; and
(c) a single handrail complying with the requirements for handrails in AS 1428.1, from the top of the entry point continuous to the bottom level area; and
(d) a level area—
  (i) 1500 mm long for the width of the zero depth entry at the entry point; and
  (ii) located at the bottom of the zero depth entry at a level between 900 mm and 1100 mm below the stationary water level.
