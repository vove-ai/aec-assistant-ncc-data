# F1D10 — Surface finishes

In a building or part of a building, the flooring or surface finish of a roof, balcony, terrace, podium, or similar part of a building must be—

(a) self-draining; or
(b) directly fixed to a membrane complying with F1D7.

> F1D10(a) does not apply to areas subject to vehicular traffic or limited trafficability stone/pebble coverings (ballast) to roof membranes
