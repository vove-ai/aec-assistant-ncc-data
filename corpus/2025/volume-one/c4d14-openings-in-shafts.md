# C4D14 — Openings in shafts

In a building of Type A construction, an opening in a wall providing access to a ventilating, pipe, garbage or other service shaft must be protected by—

(a) if it is in a sanitary compartment — a door or panel which, together with its frame, is non-combustible or has an FRL of not less than –/30/30; or
(b) a self-closing –/60/30 fire door or hopper; or
(c) an access panel having an FRL of not less than –/60/30; or
(d) if the shaft is a garbage shaft — a door or hopper of non-combustible construction.
