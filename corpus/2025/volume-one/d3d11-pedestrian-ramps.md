# D3D11 — Pedestrian ramps

**(1)** A fire-isolated ramp may be substituted for a fire-isolated stairway if the construction enclosing the ramp and the width and ceiling height comply with the requirements for a fire-isolated stairway.

**(2)** A ramp serving as a required exit must—

(a) where the ramp is also serving as an accessible ramp under Part D4, be in accordance with AS 1428.1; or
(b) in any other case, have a gradient not steeper than 1:8.

**(3)** The floor surface of a ramp, other than a threshold ramp, must have a slip-resistance classification not less than that listed in Table D3D15 when tested in accordance with AS 4586.
