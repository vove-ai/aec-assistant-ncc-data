# I18D7 — Heating appliances

The installation of a stove, heater or similar appliance in a temporary structure must as far as practicable comply with the following standards:

(a) Domestic oil-fired appliances – Installation: AS 1691.
(b) Domestic solid-fuel burning appliances – Installation: AS/NZS 2918.
(c) LP gas portable mobile appliances: AS 2658.
