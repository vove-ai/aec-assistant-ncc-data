# J1V2 — Green star

**(1)** For a Class 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9 building, compliance with NSW J1P1 is verified when—

(a) the building complies with the simulation requirements, and is registered, for a Green Star Buildings rating; and
(b) the annual greenhouse gas emissions of the proposed building are less than 90% of the annual greenhouse gas emissions of the reference building; and
(c) in the proposed building, a thermal comfort level of between a Predicted Mean Vote of -1 to +1 is achieved across not less than 95% of the floor area of all occupied zones for not less than 98% of the annual hours of operation of the building; and
(d) the building complies with the additional requirements in Specification 33.

**(2)** The calculation method used for (1) must comply with ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 140.

> Buildings using NSW J1V2 are required to calculate and demonstrate a reduction of upfront carbon in line with the NABERS Upfront Embodied Carbon methodology as part of Green Star Buildings rating.
