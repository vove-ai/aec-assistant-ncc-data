# B1P4 — Structural resistance

The structural resistance of materials and forms of construction must be determined using five percentile characteristics material properties with appropriate allowance for—

(a) known construction activities; and
(b) type of material; and
(c) characteristics of the site; and
(d) the degree of accuracy inherent in the methods used to assess the structural behaviour; and
(e) action effects arising from the differential settlement of foundations, and from restrained dimensional changes due to temperature, moisture, shrinkage, creep and similar effects.
