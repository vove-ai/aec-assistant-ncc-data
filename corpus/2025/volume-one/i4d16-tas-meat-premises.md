# I4D16 — Meat premises

Premises used for the preparation or sale of red meat, other than those licensed under the Primary Produce Safety Act 2011, that comply with—

(a) TAS Part I6; or
(b) the provisions of TAS I4D3 to TAS I4D14 and I4D18, satisfy in relation to building construction, the requirements of TAS I4P2 to TAS I4P13.
