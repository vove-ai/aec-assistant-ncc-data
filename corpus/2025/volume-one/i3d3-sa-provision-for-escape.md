# I3D3 — Provision for escape

**(1)** Exit travel distances in a farm building must comply with D2D5 except—

(a) no point on the floor must be more than 20 m from an exit, or a point from which travel in different directions to 2 exits is available, in which case the maximum distance to one of those exits must not exceed 60 m; and
(b) in a machinery room, plant room or the like, the distance to a single exit serving a storey at the level of access to a road or open space may be increased to 30 m.

**(2)** Exits that are required as alternative means of egress in a farm building must be in accordance with D2D6 except they must not be more than 80 m apart.
