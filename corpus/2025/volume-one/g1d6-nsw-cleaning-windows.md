# G1D6 — Cleaning windows

A building that includes windows located 3 or more storeys above ground level satisfies Performance Requirement NSW G1P6 where—

(a) the windows can be cleaned wholly from within the building; or
(b) provision is made for the cleaning of the windows by a method complying with the Work Health and Safety Act 2011 and Regulations made under that Act.
