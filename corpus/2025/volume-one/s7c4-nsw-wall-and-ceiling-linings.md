# S7C4 — Wall and ceiling linings

**(1)** A wall or ceiling lining system must comply with the group number specified in Table S7C4 and for buildings not fitted with a sprinkler system (other than a FPAA101D or FPAA101H system) complying with Specification 17 have—

(a) a smoke growth rate index not more than 100; or
(b) an average specific extinction area less than 250 m2/kg.

**(2)** A group number of a wall or ceiling lining and the smoke growth rate index or average specific extinction area must be determined by in accordance with AS 5637.1.

> (2) ”Specific areas” means within—
>   (a) for Class 2 and 3 buildings, a sole-occupancy unit; and
>   (b) for Class 5 buildings, open plan offices with a minimum floor dimension/floor to ceiling height ratio > 5; and
>   (c) for Class 6 buildings, shops or other building with a minimum floor dimension/floor to ceiling height ratio > 5; and
>   (d) for Class 9a health-care buildings, patient care areas; and
>   (e) for Class 9b theatres and halls, etc, an auditorium; and
>   (f) for Class 9b schools, a classroom; and
>   (g) for Class 9c buildings, resident use area.
