# F7V4 — Sound transmission through walls [F7P4(1)(a) and (2)]

Compliance with F7P4(1)(a) and (2) to avoid the transmission of airborne sound through walls is verified when it is measured in-situ that—

(a) a wall separating sole-occupancy units has a weighted standardised level difference (DnT,w) not less than 40 when determined under AS ISO 717.1; or
(b) a wall separating a sole-occupancy unit from a kitchen, bathroom, sanitary compartment (not being an associated ensuite), laundry, plant room or utilities room has a weighted standardised level difference (DnT,w) not less than 40 when determined under AS ISO 717.1.
