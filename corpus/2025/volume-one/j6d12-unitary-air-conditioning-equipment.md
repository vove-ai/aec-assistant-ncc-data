# J6D12 — Unitary air-conditioning equipment

**(1)** Water-to-air unitary air-conditioners with a rated capacity greater than or equal to 65 kW, including packaged air-conditioners, split systems, and variable refrigerant flow units, must have a minimum energy efficiency ratio of 4.0 Wr/Winput power for cooling when tested in accordance with AS/NZS ISO 13256.1 at test condition C3, where input power includes both compressor and fan input power.

**(2)** Assessed individually or collectively, a building’s air-cooled unitary air-conditioning equipment, including packaged air-conditioners, split systems, and variable refrigerant flow units, must have a weighted efficiency index greater than 1—

(a) calculated using the following formula, based on the energy efficiency ratio, WEI_(EER)=(∑_(Allapplicableunitaryconditioners)_(,i)EER_iD_i)/(∑_(Allapplicableunitaryairconditioners,i)EER_i^(min)D_i), where—
  (i) WEI_(EER) = the weighted efficiency index of the unitary air-conditioners serving a building, assessed based on their rated Energy Efficiency Ratios; and
  (ii) for each air-conditioner, i—
    (A) EER_i = the tested energy efficiency ratio, determined in accordance with MEPS; and
    (B) D_i = the rated cooling capacity determined in accordance with MEPS; and
    (C) EER_i^(min) = the applicable nominal minimum energy efficiency ratio in Table J6D12a; or
(b) calculated using the following formula, based on the Total Cooling Seasonal Performance Factor, WEI_(TCSPF)=(∑_(Allapplicableunitaryairconditioners,j)TCSPF_jD_j)/(∑_(Allapplicableunitaryairconditioners,j)TCSPF_j^(min)D_j), where—
  (i) WEI_(TCSPF) = the weighted efficiency index of the unitary air-conditioners serving a building, assessed based on their rated Total Cooling Seasonal Performance Factors; and
  (ii) for each air-conditioner, j—
    (A) TCSPF_j = the mixed zone Total Cooling Seasonal Performance Factor, determined in accordance with MEPS for the commercial based use of the product; and
    (B) D_j = the rated cooling capacity determined in accordance with MEPS; and
    (C) TCSPF_j^(min) = the applicable nominal minimum energy efficiency ratio in Table J6D12b.

**(3)** Where the weighted efficiency index contribution of an air-conditioner is assessed using the formula at (2)(a), air-cooled unitary air-conditioners, including packaged air-conditioners, split systems and variable refrigerant flow units, must have—

(a) variable speed outdoor fans; and
(b) at least one—
  (i) digital scroll compressor; or
  (ii) inverter driven compressor.

**(4)** The compressor required by 3(b) must be sized sufficiently to allow the unit to modulate across 50% to 100% of its rated cooling capacity.

**(5)** The requirements of (2) to (4) do not apply to—

(a) unitary air-conditioners with a rated cooling capacity of less than 10 kW; or
(b) unitary air-conditioners that serve only a Class 4 part of a building.

**(5)** The requirements of (2) to (4) do not apply to unitary air-conditioners with a rated cooling capacity of less than 10kW.

### Table J6D12a — Nominal minimum energy efficiency ratio figures for air-cooled unitary air-conditioning systems

| Rated cooling capacity | Unitary systems other than variable refrigerant flow units | Variable refrigerant flow units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ≤ 39 kW | 3.3 | 3.8 |
| > 39 kW | 3.1 | 3.4 |

### Table J6D12b — Nominal minimum TCSPF figures for air-cooled unitary air-conditioning systems

| Rated cooling capacity | Unitary systems other than variable refrigerant flow units |
| --- | --- |
| ≤ 30 kW | 3.6 |
| > 30 kW | 3.5 |

> Variable refrigerant flow and multi-split units are tested as representative combination.
