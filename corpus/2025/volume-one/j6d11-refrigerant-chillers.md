# J6D11 — Refrigerant chillers

**(1)** Refrigerant chillers must—

(a) individually, comply with (2); or
(b) collectively, comply with (3).

**(2)** When assessed in accordance with AHRI 551/591, a chiller must—

(a) where air-cooled—
  (i) in climate zones 1 or 3, achieve a full load operation energy efficiency ratio of not less than 3.1; and
  (ii) in climate zones 2, 4, 5, 6 or 7 achieve a full load operation energy efficiency ratio and an integrated part load value such that the sum of the two is not less than—
    (A) 8.1, where the rated cooling capacity of the chiller is not greater than 550 kW; and
    (B) 8.5, where the rated cooling capacity of the chiller is greater than 550 kW; and
  (iii) in climate zone 8, achieve an integrated part load value of not less than—
    (A) 5.1, where the rated cooling capacity of the chiller is not greater than 550 kW; or
    (B) 5.4, where the rated cooling capacity of the chiller is greater than 550 kW; and
(b) where water-cooled—
  (i) in climate zones 1 or 2, achieve full load operation energy efficiency ratio of not less than the value specified in Table J6D11a; and
  (ii) in climate zones 3 to 8, achieve a full load operation energy efficiency ratio and an integrated part load value such that the sum of the two is not less than the sum of the full load and integrated part load values specified in Table J6D11a, as applicable.

**(3)** A connected group of one or more refrigerant chillers, used as part of an air-conditioning system, must achieve a climate specific part load value, determined in accordance with Specification 47, greater than—

(a) the applicable value specified in Table J6D11b, where all chillers in the group are water-cooled; or
(b) the applicable value specified in Table J6D11c, where all chillers in the group are air-cooled; or
(c) the average of the applicable values specified in Table J6D11b and Table J6D11c weighted according to the total full-load capacity of the chillers, where the group has both water-cooled and air-cooled chillers.

**(4)** The requirements of (1), (2) and (3) do not apply to—

(a) 4-pipe chillers; or
(b) air-source heat pump water heaters.
