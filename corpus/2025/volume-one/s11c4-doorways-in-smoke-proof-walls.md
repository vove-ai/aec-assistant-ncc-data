# S11C4 — Doorways in smoke-proof walls

A door required by C3D6 or this Specification to be smoke-proof or have an FRL must provide a smoke reservoir by not extending within 400 mm of the underside of—

(a) a roof covering; or
(b) the floor above; or
(c) an imperforate false ceiling that will prevent the free passage of smoke.
