# J6D8 — Pump systems

**(1)** General — Pumps and pipework that form part of an air-conditioning system must either—

(a) separately comply with (2), (3), (4), (5), (6) and (7); or
(b) achieve a pump motor power per unit of flowrate lower than the pump motor power per unit of flowrate achieved when applying (2), (3), (4), (5), (6) and (7) together.

**(2)** Where the pump motor power input at the duty point is greater than 750 W, a pump must be capable of—

(a) variable speed operation; and
(b) where serving a distributive system—
  (i) variable volume operation; and
  (ii) variable pressure operation to the extent permitted by—
    (A) static head requirements; and
    (B) the requirements of downstream equipment.

**(3)** A distributive system must not have—

(a) uncontrolled bypasses; or
(b) three port valves.

**(4)** The requirements of (3)(b) do not apply to a distributive system with a single chiller, including a chiller used for space heating, that serves—

(a) air-conditioning systems with a total co-incident design load of less than 200 kW; or
(b) only one heating coil or one cooling coil.

**(5)** Circulator pumps — A glandless impeller pump, with a rated hydraulic power output of less than 2.5 kW and that is used in closed loop systems must have an energy efficiency index (EEI) not more than 0.27 calculated in accordance with European Union Commission Regulation No. 622/2012.

**(6)** Other pumps — Pumps that are in accordance with Articles 1 and 2 of European Union Commission Regulation No. 547/2012 must have a minimum efficiency index (MEI) of 0.4 or more when calculated in accordance with European Union Commission Regulation No. 547/2012.

**(7)** Pipework — Straight segments of pipework along the index run, forming part of an air-conditioning system—

(a) in pipework systems that do not have branches and have the same flow rate throughout the entire pipe network, must achieve an average pressure drop of not more than—
  (i) for constant flow systems, the values nominated in Table J6D8a; or
  (ii) for variable flow systems, the values nominated in Table J6D8b; or
(b) in any other pipework system, must achieve an average pressure drop of not more than—
  (i) for constant flow systems, the values nominated in Table J6D8c; or
  (ii) for variable flow systems, the values nominated in Table J6D8d.

**(8)** The requirements of (7) do not apply—

(a) to valves and fittings; or
(b) where the smallest pipe size compliant with (7) results in a velocity of 0.7 m/s or less at design flow.
