# E1D6 — Where sprinklers are required: Class 2 and 3 buildings other than residential care buildings

**(1)** Sprinklers are required throughout—

(a) A shared accommodation building; and
(b) In a Class 2 or 3 building, or any multi-classified building containing a Class 2 or 3 part, if any part of the building has—
  (i) a rise in storeys of 4 or more; and
  (ii) an effective height of not more than 25 m.

**(2)** The requirements of (1) do not apply to—

(a) a residential care building; and
(b) a Class 8 electricity network substation with a floor area not more than 200 m2 located within a multi-classified building.
