# S16C4 — Platform swimming pool lift

A platform swimming pool lift must be—

(a) capable of being operated from the swimming pool surround, within the swimming pool, and on the platform; and
(b) located where the water depth is not more than 1300 mm; and
(c) designed to withstand a weight capacity of not less than 160 kg and be capable of sustaining a static load of not less than 1.5 times the rated load.
