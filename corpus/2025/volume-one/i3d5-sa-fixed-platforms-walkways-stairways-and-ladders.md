# I3D5 — Fixed platforms, walkways, stairways and ladders

A fixed platform, stairway, ladder and any going and riser, landing, handrail or barrier may comply with AS 1657 in lieu of D3D14, D3D15, D3D17 to D3D21 and D3D22 where it serves a farm building.
