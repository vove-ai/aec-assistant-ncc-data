# J8D4 — Swimming pool heating and pumping

**(1)** Heating for a swimming pool must be by—

(a) a solar heater; or
(b) a heater using reclaimed heat from another process such as reject heat from a refrigeration plant; or
(c) a geothermal heater; or
(d) a gas heater that achieves a minimum gross thermal efficiency of 90%; or
(e) a heat pump; or
(f) a combination of (a) to (e).

**(2)** Where some or all of the heating required by (1) is by a gas heater or a heat pump, the swimming pool must have—

(a) a cover with a minimum R-Value of 0.05; and
(b) a time switch to control the operation of the heater.

**(3)** A time switch must be provided to control the operation of a circulation pump for a swimming pool.

**(4)** Where required, a time switch must be capable of switching electric power on and off at variable pre-programmed times and on variable pre-programmed days.

**(5)** Pipework carrying heated or chilled water for a swimming pool must comply with the insulation requirements of J6D9.

**(6)** A building with gas heated water for a swimming pool must be provided with—

(a) reserved electrical capacity sufficient to support future installation of air-source heat pump water heating equipment for the swimming pool assuming a nominal coefficient of performance of 3.25; and
(b) reserved space within the main electrical distribution board comprising at least one empty three-phase circuit breaker slot and 4 DIN rail spaces per 35 kW of water heating capacity, labelled to indicate reservation for future installation of water heating equipment for the swimming pool; and
(c) reserved space in the electrical riser or supply pathway for future swimming pool heating equipment sized in accordance with (a); and
(d) reserved plant area with—
  (i) space to accommodate heating plant of 7 m² plus 0.04 m²/kW of heating capacity; and
  (ii) sufficient ventilation openings to achieve airflow of 115 L/s per kW of heating capacity with no recirculation of air; and
(e) a hot water circulation system that operates at a peak temperature of less than 50°C.

**QLD variation (REPLACE) — Swimming pool heating and pumping**

**(6)** This sub-clause has deliberately been left blank.

**(7)** The capacity and space required by (6) must be sufficient to serve the same design load used to specify any gas-powered swimming pool water heating equipment.

**QLD variation (REPLACE) — Swimming pool heating and pumping**

**(7)** This sub-clause has deliberately been left blank.

**(8)** For the purpose of J8D4, a swimming pool does not include a spa pool.
