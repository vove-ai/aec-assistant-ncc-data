# I3D1 — Application of Part

**(1)** The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part apply to farm buildings in South Australia.

**(2)** The Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of this Part take precedence where there is a difference to the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of Sections C, D, E and F.

> Part I3 contains Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions additional to those contained in Sections C, D, E and F for farm buildings.
>
> The standard NCC definition and provisions for farm sheds do not apply in South Australia. All buildings that meet the SA definition of farm buildings must comply with Part I3.
