# J9D5 — Facilities for solar photovoltaic and battery systems

**(1)** The main electrical switchboard of a building must contain at least 2 empty three-phrase circuit breaker slots and 4 DIN rail spaces labelled to indicate the use of each space for a battery system.

**(2)** A building must be provided with an on-site solar photovoltaic system—

(a) that covers 100% of the roof area, excluding areas—
  (i) shaded for more than 25% of annual daylight hours; or
  (ii) where the roof pitch exceeds 45° relative to horizontal; or
  (iii) that are awnings; or
  (iv) that are car parks; or
  (v) that are roof gardens; or
  (vi) that are terraces or other rooftop areas designated for occupation; or
  (vii) used for roof lights or translucent roofing for natural lighting or the like; or
  (viii) designated as a trafficable area, access for height safety systems or plant maintenance access or a required path of travel to an exit; or
  (ix) used for plant equipment, services installations or the like; or
  (x) above exhaust air flows from heat rejection equipment or the like; or
  (xi) where provision of solar photovoltaic panel is impractical on account of panel size or mounting system limitations; or
(b) with a peak system output rating per m2 of building conditioned space not less than those specified in Table J9D5a.

**(3)** In addition to (2), a building where gas is used for building services must be provided with further on-site photovoltaic system peak output capacity not less than the value specified in Table J9D5b.

**(4)** If the on-site solar photovoltaic system in (3) is placed on a building roof, the panels must—

(a) not be shaded for more than 25% of daylight hours; and
(b) not have a pitch of more than 45°.

> (1) The requirements of J9D5(2) do not apply to a building—
>   (a) where 100% of the effective roof area is shaded for more than 70% of annual daylight hours; or
>   (b) provided with an on-site renewable energy generation system with a generation capacity at least equivalent to a solar photovoltaic system described under J9D5(2).
> (2) The requirements of J9D5(1) do not apply to a building with battery systems installed.

### Table J9D5a — Minimum solar photovoltaic system installed peak output (W) per m2 of conditioned space for buildings without gas

| Building Class | Climate zone 1 | Climate zones 2 to 7 | Climate zone 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class 5, 6, and 7 | 95 | 65 | 55 |
| Class 3 and 9 | 70 | 40 | 35 |

### Table J9D5b — Minimum solar photovoltaic system installed peak output (W) per m2 of conditioned space for buildings with gas, in addition to the requirements of J9D5(2)

| Building Class | Climate zones 1 and 3 | Climate zones 2 and 5 | Climate zones 4 and 6 | Climate zone 7 | Climate zone 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class 5, 6 and 7 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1.7 | 4.7 |
| Class 3 and 9 | 2.6 | 0.9 | 2.6 | 0.9 | 0 |

> Where compliance with J9D5 leads to the installation of an on-site solar photovoltaic system, existing network connection rules, governed by the Distribution Network Service Provider (DNSP), will apply. Depending on the size of the system and the amount of PV already installed in the local area, the DNSP may require a building to have certain features that help maintain local electricity grid stability. These could include options for a dynamic grid connection, demand response capability as per J9D3(6) for key energy using equipment, or on-site energy storage.
