# I4D9 — Emergency lighting requirements

**(1)** An emergency lighting system complying with E4D2 must be installed in a bulk grain storage facility except that—

(a) emergency lighting is not required at ground floor level in cell type silos or storage sheds; and
(b) the spacing of emergency lights can be increased to twice the distance required for Class 7 building.

**(2)** An emergency lighting system complying with E4D2 must be installed in an underground passage in a bulk grain storage facility.
