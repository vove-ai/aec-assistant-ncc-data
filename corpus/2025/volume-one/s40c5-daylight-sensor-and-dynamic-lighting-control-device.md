# S40C5 — Daylight sensor and dynamic lighting control device

A daylight sensor and dynamic control device for artificial lighting must, in response to the measured level of available daylight in the range 50–1000 lux—

(a) for high pressure discharge lights, switch lights on and off with a programmable delay and switching differential; and
(b) for all other lights, dim continuously or step lighting output down in not less than 4 steps so that the lowest power setting is less than 50% of full power.
