# B7P1 — Contamination

**(1)** Rainwater must be stored in a way that reduces the likelihood of contamination.

**(2)** Stored rainwater must not pose a hazard to public health.

> B7P1 does not apply to sites not provided with a drinking water service by a Network Utility Operator.
