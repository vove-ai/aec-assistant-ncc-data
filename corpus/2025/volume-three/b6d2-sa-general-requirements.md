# B6D2 — General requirements

A rainwater service must comply with Section 15 of AS/NZS 3500.1 with the following variation—

(d) For Class 1 buildings and extensions or additions to Class 1 buildings the following applies.

Where a drinking water is used to top-up a rainwater tank or supplement a rainwater service, it shall be maintained by an automatic change-over device, automatic top-up device or a device/mechanism that facilitates an automatic switching without manual intervention.
