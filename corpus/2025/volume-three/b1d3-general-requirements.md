# B1D3 — General requirements

**(1)** A cold water service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1.

**(2)** Notwithstanding (1), plastic pipes and fittings must not be used above-ground in combined sprinkler and hydrant systems.

**QLD variation (INSERT) — General requirements**

**(3)** Notwithstanding (1), Appendix I of AS/NZS 3500.1 is adopted as an Informative appendix.

**QLD variation (INSERT) — General requirements**

**(4)** For clause 2.5.3(c) of AS/NZS 3500.1, the minimum pressure rating for plastic pipes used in a cold water service is PN 12.
