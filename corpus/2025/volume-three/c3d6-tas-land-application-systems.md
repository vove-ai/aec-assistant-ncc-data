# C3D6 — Land application systems

**(1)** An On-site wastewater management system and associated land application system must avoid the following:

(a) Damage from root penetration, superimposed loads or ground movement.
(b) Entry of water, foul air or gasses from the system into buildings.
(c) Entry of stormwater into the system.
(d) Blockage and unintended or uncontrolled discharge.

**(2)** An On-site wastewater management system and associated land application system must provide the following:

(a) Protection against internal contamination.
(b) Access for maintenance and cleaning.
(c) Ventilation to avoid foul air and gasses accumulation in the system.

**(3)** A facility must continue to satisfy the requirements of C3D6(1) and C3D6(2).
