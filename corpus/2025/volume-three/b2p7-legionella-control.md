# B2P7 — Legionella control

Heated water must be stored and delivered under conditions which avoid the likelihood of the growth of a Legionella bacteria count greater than or equal to 10 Legionella colony forming units (cfu) per millilitre.

> A risk assessment should be undertaken for the control and management of Legionella in heated water systems in aged care, health-care and other similar facilities which likely contain immunocompromised occupants.
