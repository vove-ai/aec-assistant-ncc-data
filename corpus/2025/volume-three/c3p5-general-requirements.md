# C3P5 — General requirements

On-site wastewater management systems that facilitate on-site storage, treatment, disposal or re-use of wastewater must be designed and constructed—

(a) with required treatment and storage capacity for the volume and make up of waste and frequency of discharge for disposal; and
(b) with required size, strength and rigidity for the nature, flow rates, volume of wastes and/or waste products which must be processed; and
(c) using materials which are impervious both to the waste for which disposal is required and to water; and
(d) to avoid the likelihood of surface water and stormwater entering the system.
