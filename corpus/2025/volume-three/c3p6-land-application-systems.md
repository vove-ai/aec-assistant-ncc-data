# C3P6 — Land application systems

**(1)** On-site wastewater management systems and associated land application systems must—

(a) complete the treatment, uptake and absorption of the final effluent within the boundaries of the approved area; and
(b) protect against internal contamination; and
(c) provide ventilation to avoid the likelihood of foul air and gases from accumulating in the system.

**(2)** On-site wastewater management systems and associated land application systems must avoid the likelihood of—

(a) the creation of unpleasant odours or the accumulation of offensive matter; and
(b) stormwater run-off entering the system; and
(c) root penetration or ingress of ground water entering the system; and
(d) unintended or uncontrolled discharge; and
(e) blockage and leakage; and
(f) damage from superimposed loads or ground movement.
