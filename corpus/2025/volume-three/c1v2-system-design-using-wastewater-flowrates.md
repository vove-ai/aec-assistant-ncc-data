# C1V2 — System design using wastewater flowrates

**(1)** Compliance with C1P5 for pipe sizing is verified for the plumbing design when—

(a) fully vented systems and fully vented modified systems are designed in accordance with Section 8 of AS/NZS 3500.2: and
(b) single stack systems and single stack modified systems are designed in accordance with Section 9 of AS/NZS 3500.2: and
(c) consideration by the designer is given to expanding the exclusion zones referenced in AS/NZS 3500.2 (1 m exclusion zone at the base of the stack) for high rise buildings.

**(2)** For the purposes of (1), for each pipework section, the probable simultaneous wastewater flowrate determined through C1V1 may be converted to an equivalent AS/NZS 3500.2 Fixture Unit (FU) in accordance with the following:

FU_(Total)=6.75Q^2_(Total)

**(3)** In the equation shown at (2):

(a) Q = the wastewater flowrate (L/s); and
(b) FU = the equivalent fixture units converted from a flowrate (dimensionless) where the result is rounded up to the next integer.

**(4)** For the purposes of (1), all relevant sizing tables and design guidance referencing FU in AS/NZS 3500.2 may alternatively be converted to an equivalent flowrate using the following:

Q=√((ΣFU)/(6.75))

**(5)** In the equation shown at (4), ΣFU = the sum of fixture units to be converted (dimensionless).

> The conversion of the probable simultaneous wastewater flowrate into fixture units (FU) using the formula in (2) allows the resultant (FU) value to be used when applying the relevant sizing tables and design guidance in the applicable sections of AS/NZS 3500.2 in accordance with (1).
