# B1P5 — Water efficiency

Where a cold water service is used, efficient use of drinking water must be achieved by—

(a) incorporation of—
  (i) a tap or outlet for a shower, basin, kitchen sink or laundry trough, to a flow rate of not more than 9 l/m; and
  (ii) a cistern or flushing device for a urinal, to a flush volume of not more than 2.5 litres for each—
    (A) single urinal stall; or
    (B) 600 mm length of a continuous urinal wall; and
  (iii) a dual flush cistern or flushing valve that is connected to a water closet pan to a flush volume of not more than—
    (A) 6 and 3 litres; or
    (B) 4.5 and 3 litres; or
(b) water saving measures equivalent to or greater than those described in (a).

> The flush volumes of B1P5 may be within a tolerance of—
>
> (a) ±0.5 litres for the full flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> (b) +0.5 litres for the reduced flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> (c) ±0.2 litres for a 4.5/3 litre cistern.

> The requirements of B1P5 do not apply to a vacuum drainage system.
