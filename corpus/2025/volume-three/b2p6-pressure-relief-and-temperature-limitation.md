# B2P6 — Pressure relief and temperature limitation

Containers used for producing and/or storing heated water are to relieve excessive pressure and avoid flash steam production by—

(a) relieving pressure so that the maximum rated working pressure, or 1400 kPa, whichever is the lesser, is not exceeded; and
(b) limiting water temperatures to a maximum of 99°C; or
(c) other suitable means providing an equivalent level of safety to (a) and (b).

> B2P6(a) has a tolerance of +5% or 14 kPa whichever is the greater.
