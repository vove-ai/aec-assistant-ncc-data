# B4P3 — Isolation

**(1)** A means of isolation must be provided to a fire-fighting water service and components that require testing, maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

**(2)** Adequate drainage must be provided for flow testing of fire-fighting water services.

> Isolation valves are a means of providing isolation.
