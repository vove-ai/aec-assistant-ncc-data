# B3P8 — Water Efficiency

Where a non-drinking water service is used for flushing toilets or urinals, efficient use of non-drinking water must be achieved by—

(a) Incorporation of—
  (i) a cistern or flushing device for a urinal, to a flush volume of not more than 2.5 litres for each—
    (A) single urinal stall; or
    (B) 600mm length of a continuous urinal wall; and
  (ii) a dual flush cistern or flushing valve that is connected to a water closet pan, to a flush volume of not more than—
    (A) 6 and 3 litres; or
    (B) 4.5 and 3 litres; or
(b) water saving measures equivalent to or greater than those described in (a).

> In New South Wales, fixtures, tapware and appliances installed within new Class 1 and 2 Buildings are subject to the water efficiency targets regulated under BASIX

> The flush volumes of B3P8 may be within a tolerance of—
>
> (a) ±0.5 litres for the full flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> (b) ±0.5 litres for the reduced flush of a 6/3 litre cistern; or
> (c) ±0.5 litres for a 4.5/3 litre cistern

> The requirements of B3P8 do not apply to a vacuum drainage system
