# B5D3 — Non-Drinking Water Service

**(1)** A non-drinking water service or supply may have a cross-connection to another separate non-drinking water service on the same site provided it is protected with a low hazard device. The low hazard device must be selected and installed in accordance with Section 4 of AS/NZS 3500.1

**(2)** Where a site is served by a Network Utility Operator’s drinking water supply, appropriate containment protection must be:

(a) selected and installed with the Network Utility Operator relevant regulations, guidelines, policies or similar documents; or
(b) where the Network Utility Operator does not prescribe requirements for containment protection, assign a containment protection Hazard Rating in accordance with B5D7(10), B5D7(11) or B5D7(12), and select and install appropriate containment protection in accordance with Section 4 of AS/NZS 3500.1

> Where a Containment Protection backflow prevention device is installed external of a Network Utility Operator water meter, the valve remains the responsibility of the property owner unless owned by the Network Utility Operator, including any testing, servicing and maintenance that may be required.
>
> Where the Network Utility Operator prescribes containment protection hazard ratings above the hazard rating set out in B5D7(10), B5D7(11), and B5D7(12), the Network Utility Operator is responsible for the regulation of the containment backflow installation.

> B5D3 is not applicable to non-drinking water service used for agricultural purposes where the non-drinking water service is supplied solely by non-drinking water service(s)and is fully independent from any other water service.

> For the purposes of B5D3, a ‘separate’ non-drinking water service exists where multiple non-drinking water services are on the same site and each is supplied by water from different sources.
>
> For example, on a site connected to both recycled water and bore water, both may be considered non-drinking water services and are supplied by different sources. Therefore, each is a ‘separate’ non-drinking water service.
