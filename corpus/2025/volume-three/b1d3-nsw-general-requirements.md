# B1D3 — General requirements

**(1)** A cold water service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1, with the following variations

(a) Clause 5.4.2: insert subclause (m) Where valves are located below ground within the property boundary, they shall be provided with a surface box and riser. The box lid shall be permanently marked with a “W”.

**(2)** Notwithstanding (1), plastic pipes and fittings must not be used in a cold water service above-ground to supply combined sprinkler and hydrant systems.
