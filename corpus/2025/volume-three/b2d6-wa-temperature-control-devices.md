# B2D6 — Temperature control devices

**(1)** A temperature control device used to deliver heated water in accordance with WA B2D5(1)(a) must be a—

(a) thermostatic mixing valve; or
(b) thermostatically controlled tap.

**(2)** A temperature control device used to deliver heated water in accordance with WA B2D5(1)(b) must be—

(a) thermostatic mixing valve; or
(b) thermostatically controlled tap; or
(c) tempering valve; or
(d) temperature limited water heater.

**(3)** The required maximum delivery temperature must be achieved in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.4.

> WA B2D6 applies to heated water installations to which WA B2D5 applies.
