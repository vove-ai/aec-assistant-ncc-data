# B5D3 — Non-drinking water service

**(1)** A hazard exists wherever it is possible for water or contaminants to enter a non-drinking water service or supply via any potential or actual cross-connection between itself and another separate non-drinking water service on the same site.

**(2)** Each hazard must—

(a) be assigned a Hazard Rating in accordance with B5D7(10), B5D7(11) or B5D7(12); and
(b) be isolated from the non-drinking water service by appropriate protection which is selected and installed in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1.

**(3)** If a drinking water service is interconnected with a non-drinking water service for the purpose of emergency backup or to supplement, the drinking water supply shall be fitted with a high hazard backflow prevention device at the point of interconnection as a zone protection.

> The Office of the Technical Regulator has produced a document ‘Guidelines for Non-drinking water in South Australia’. The guidance document has been designed for the plumbing and water industries, water industry entities, and property owners with a non-drinking water supply.
>
> The guidelines outline the requirements and responsibilities for installing, operating, and maintaining non-drinking water systems per the Water Industry Act 2012, Water Industry Regulations 2012, and appropriate technical standards.
