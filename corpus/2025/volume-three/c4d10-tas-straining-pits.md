# C4D10 — Straining pits

**(1)** The straining pit must be connected and authorised by the Permit Authority.

**(2)** The straining pit basket must—

(a) be constructed of mild steel wire with a minimum diameter of 2.5 mm woven to give 7 mm aperture widths on a 32 mm x 32 mm x 25 mm angle iron frame with mitred corners; or
(b) be constructed from minimum 3 mm thick mild steel plate, with 6 holes at 12 mm centres over the entire area of the basket; and
(c) be hot dipped galvanised after fabrication.
