# B1D5 — Cold water fire sprinkler systems

A cold water service used for fire-fighting must be in accordance with—

(a) the requirements of E1 of Volume One, where applicable; and
(b) FPAA 101D when installed in a Class 2 or Class 3 building with an effective height of not more than 25 m and a rise in storeys of 4 or more; and
(c) AS/NZS 3500.1.

> B1D5(b) does not apply to a residential care building.

> For all other fire-fighting water services, see Part B4.
