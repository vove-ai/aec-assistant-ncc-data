# C1P6 — Contamination

**(1)** The entry of water, foul gases, sewerage and sullage from a sanitary plumbing system into buildings must be avoided.

**(2)** The entry of foul gases from a sanitary plumbing system into buildings must be avoided, such that—

(a) at pressures of up to ±375 Pa, water trap seals will not be reduced to depths less than
  (i) 70 mm for trap seals in pressurised rooms; and
  (ii) 25 mm for all other applications; or
(b) an equivalent level of safety to human health is achieved as a system complying with (a).

**(3)** The entry of surface water, sub-surface water and stormwater into a sanitary plumbing system must be avoided.
