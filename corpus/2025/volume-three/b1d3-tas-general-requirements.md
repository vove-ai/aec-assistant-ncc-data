# B1D3 — General requirements

**(1)** A cold water service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1.

**(2)** Notwithstanding (1), plastic pipes and fittings must not be used above-ground in combined sprinkler and hydrant systems.

**(3)** Cold water tanks forming part of a drinking water supply must comply with TAS B1D7.

**(4)** Backflow prevention for boundary containment must comply with the Network Utility Operator Boundary Backflow Containment Selection Requirements.

> B1D3(1) does not apply to existing low-pressure gravity only water supply systems.
