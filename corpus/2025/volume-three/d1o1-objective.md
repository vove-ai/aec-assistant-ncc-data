# D1O1 — Objective

The Objective of this Part is to—

(a) safeguard people from illness, injury or loss (including loss of amenity) due to excessive noise of a plumbing and drainage system; and
(b) ensure that a plumbing and drainage system installation throughout its serviceable life will continue to satisfy the requirements of Objective (a).
