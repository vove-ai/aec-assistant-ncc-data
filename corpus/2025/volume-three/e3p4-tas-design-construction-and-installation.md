# E3P4 — Design, construction and installation

**(1)** A roof drainage system must ensure that—

(a) stormwater is transferred to a Network Utility Operator's stormwater system or an approved disposal system; and
(b) appropriate access is provided for maintenance and clearing blockages.

**(2)** A roof drainage installation must avoid blockage and uncontrolled discharge causing—

(a) loss of amenity; and
(b) loss to building and property.

**(3)** A roof drainage installation must avoid foul air and gasses accumulating in the roof drainage system.
