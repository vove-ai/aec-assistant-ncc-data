# B2D2 — Water heater in a heated water supply system

In New South Wales energy efficiency is regulated under the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 and Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021, Environmental Planning and Assessment (Development Certification and Fire Safety) Regulation 2021 and State Environmental Planning Policy (Sustainable Buildings 2022)

Compliance is not required with the national provisions of B2D2 as the sources of energy for new Class 1 and 10 buildings are regulated under BASIX.
