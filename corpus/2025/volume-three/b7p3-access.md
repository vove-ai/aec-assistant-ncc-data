# B7P3 — Access

Access must be provided to components of rainwater storage that require maintenance or replacement, including mechanical devices and controls.

> B7P3 does not apply to sites not provided with a drinking water service by a Network Utility Operator.

> Access to mechanical components may require removal of an access panel, cover, door, or similar obstruction.
