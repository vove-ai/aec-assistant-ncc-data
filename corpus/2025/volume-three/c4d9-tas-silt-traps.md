# C4D9 — Silt traps

**(1)** The silt trap must be connected and authorised by the Permit Authority.

**(2)** The silt trap bucket must—

(a) be constructed of mild steel plate, minimum 3 mm thick with 5 rows of 10 mm diameter holes at 25 mm centres; and
(b) be hot dipped galvanised after fabrication.
