# E3P4 — Design, construction and installation

**(1)** A roof drainage system must ensure the following—

(a) Stormwater is transferred to a point of connection.
(b) Access for maintenance and clearing blockages.

**(2)** A roof drainage installation must avoid the following—

(a) Loss of amenity due to blockages and uncontrolled discharge.
(b) Foul air and gases accumulating in the roof drainage system.
(c) Loss to buildings and property amenity due to blockages and uncontrolled discharge.
