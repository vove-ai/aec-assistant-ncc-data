# C2D4 — General requirements

A sanitary drainage system must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.2 with the following variations:

(a) Substitute AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 2.3(a) as follows: Bends in pipes shall have a throat radius complying with Table 5.6 and Figure B6 of AS/NZS 1260 (2017) and shall be free from wrinkling and flattening.
(b) Substitute AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 2.6.2(c) as follows: Vitrified clay pipes and fittings shall not be used above ground. Vitrified clay pipes and fittings shall not be used for drains under buildings unless they have been renovated using a cured in-place pipe (CIPP) techniques in accordance with Clause 3.16.3.
(c) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 3.9.1(g) insert (h) and (i) as follows: (h) at the downstream end of a trade waste pre-treatment equipment, close to the outlet; and (i) at the upstream end of a trade waste drain connected to trade waste pre-treatment equipment and where required by the Network Utility Operator, fitted with a wind driven turbine ventilator at the termination unless otherwise authorised by the receiving Network Utility Operator or authority.
(d) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 3.9.3.1(e) add (f) and (g) as follows: (f) where required by Clause 3.9.1(h) the downstream vent on any trade waste drain shall not be less than DN80; and (g) where required by Clause 3.9.1(g) the upstream vent on a trade waste drain shall not be less than DN80.
(e) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 4.4.1 insert the following: An inspection shaft or inspection point provided by the Network Utility Operator outside or within the allotment boundary satisfies the requirements of Clause 4.4.
(f) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 4.6.7.4 insert the following: The size of waste pipes from fixtures connecting into floor waste gullies shall comply with Table 6.3(A).
(g) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 4.7.1(h), insert clause 4.7.1(i) as follows: Inspection openings indicated in 4.7.1(b), (d), (e) and (f) shall be raised to finished surface level. All other inspection openings need not be raised provided they are not below paved, concreted or similar finished surfaces.
(h) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 4.7.3(c) insert clause 4.7.3.1 as follows: Where inspection openings are installed, they shall be downstream of a fixture where practical.
(i) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 12.7(g), insert clause 12.7(h) as follows: Buried pumped discharge pipes and rising mains shall be placed in close-fitting durable sleeving or continually spirally wrapped. The sleeving or spiral wrapping shall be identified for the full length with the following statement: "sewer rising main" or "pumped discharge pipe" in accordance with AS 1345.
(j) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 12.7(h), insert clause 12.7(i) as follows: Above ground pumped discharge pipes and rising mains shall be permanently identified with the following statement: "sewer rising main" or "pumped discharge pipe" in accordance with AS 1345. The statement shall be installed in a visible position running longitudinally, at no more than 3 metre intervals.
(k) After AS/NZS 3500.2 clause 13.27.2 insert clause 13.28 as follows: All trade waste pre-treatment equipment shall connect to the sanitary drainage system via a disconnector gully. Where discharge to the disconnector gully will cause the accumulation of foul gases the disconnector gully riser shall be sealed with a removable airtight cover and vented with a DN50 vent pipe in accordance with Clause 6.9.4.
(l) After AS/NZS 3500.2 Clause 6.9.3(d), insert (e). The vent from a sealed disconnector gully as required in Clause 13.28 may interconnect with the required downstream arrestor vent at a minimum of 300 mm above the overflow level of the lowest fixture connected to the trade waste pre-treatment device.

> It is illegal to discharge unauthorised liquid trade waste. Trade waste authorisation is granted by the receiving Network Utility Operator or authority having jurisdiction.
>
> Liquid Trade waste generated from industry, business, trade or manufacturing must be treated to the required standards of the authority having jurisdiction or receiving Network Utility Operator.
>
> The Office of the Technical Regulator regulates plumbing work in South Australia under the Water Industry Act 2012, including sanitary plumbing and drainage systems for liquid trade waste.
