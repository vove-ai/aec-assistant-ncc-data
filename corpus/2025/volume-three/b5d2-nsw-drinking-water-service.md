# B5D2 — Drinking water service

**(1)** A hazard exists wherever it is possible for water or contaminants to enter a drinking water service or supply via any potential or actual cross-connection between—

(a) The drinking water service; and
(b) Any of the following:
  (i) A non-drinking water service.
  (ii) A rainwater service.
  (iii) An alternative water supply,.
  (iv) A swimming pool.
  (v) Pipes, fixtures or specialist equipment (including boilers and pumps) containing chemicals, liquids, gases or other substances which may be harmful to health or safety.

**(2)** Each hazard must—

(a) Be assigned—
  (i) an Individual protection Hazard Rating in accordance with B5D7(4), (5),or (6); or
  (ii) a Zone protection Hazard Rating in accordance with B5D7(7), (8), (9); and
(b) Be isolated from the drinking water service by an appropriate backflow prevention device which is selected and installed in accordance with Section 4 of AS/NZS 3500.1.

**(3)** Where a site is served by a Network Utility Operator’s drinking water, appropriate containment protection must be:

(a) selected and installed in accordance with the Network Utility Operators relevant regulations, guidelines, policies or similar documents; or
(b) where the Network Utility Operator does not prescribe requirements for containment protection, assign a Containment protection Hazard Rating in accordance with B5D7 (10), (11) or (12), and select and install appropriate containment protection in accordance with Section 4 of AS/NZS 3500.1

> Where a Containment protection backflow prevention device is installed external of a Network Utility Operator water meter, the valve remains the responsibility of the property owner unless owned by the Network Utility Operator, including any testing, servicing and maintenance that may be required.
>
> Where the Network Utility Operator prescribes containment protection hazard ratings above the hazard rating set out in B5D7 (10), (11), and (12), the Network Utility Operator is responsible for the regulation of the containment backflow installation.

> No additional individual or zone protection backflow prevention protection is required where cross-connection mitigation is provided in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.1 as an integral part of a fixture, appliance or apparatus and is suitable for the Hazard Rating specified in B5D7.
