# B5D6 — Rainwater

When drinking water is used to top up a rainwater tank or supplement a rainwater service through the use of changeover device:

(a) it must be assigned a hazard rating based on the following building and rainwater tank types:
  (i) zone protected from above ground, buried or partially buried rainwater tank in Class 1 buildings, using a low hazard device; or
  (ii) zone protected for all other classes of buildings using:
    (A) High hazard device for buried rainwater tanks,
    (B) Low Hazard for partially buried rainwater tanks, and
    (C) Low Hazard for above ground rainwater tanks
(b) devices are to be selected and installed in accordance with Section 4 of AS/NZS 3500.1:

> - When drinking water is not connected to rainwater storage or a rainwater service, backflow prevention is not required
> - The water supply used to top up a rainwater tank may be from a Network Utility Operator drinking water supply or an alternative water supply.
> - A Network Utility Operator may prescribe backflow prevention requirements that exceed those contained in B5D6. In these circumstances those Network Utility Operator Requirements should be followed
> - Non-drinking water may be used to top up a rainwater tank, or supplement a rainwater service, however it must comply with the requirements set out in Parts B3 and B5D3
