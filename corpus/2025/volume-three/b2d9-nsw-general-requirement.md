# B2D9 — General Requirement

A heated water service must be in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.4, with the following variations:

(a) Insert clause 3.4 Tempering Valve
(b) A tempering valve shall:
  (i) have unions or similar couplings on the inlets and outlets; and
  (ii) be accessible for adjustment, maintenance or replacement.
