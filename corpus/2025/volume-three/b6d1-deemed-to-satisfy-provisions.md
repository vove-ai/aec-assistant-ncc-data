# B6D1 — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements B6P1 to B6P7 are satisfied by complying with B6D2 to B6D7.

**VIC variation (REPLACE) — Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions**

**(1)** Where a Deemed-to-Satisfy Solution is proposed, Performance Requirements B6P1 to VIC B6P8 are satisfied by complying with B6D2 to VIC B6D8.

**(2)** Where a Performance Solution is proposed, the relevant Performance Requirements must be determined in accordance with A2G2(3) and A2G4(3) as applicable.
