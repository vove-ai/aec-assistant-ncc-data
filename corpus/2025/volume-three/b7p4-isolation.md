# B7P4 — Isolation

Means of isolation must be provided to a rainwater storage and components that require testing, maintenance, or replacement including mechanical devices, appliances and controls.

> B7P4 does not apply to site not provided with a drinking water service by a Network Utility Operator.

> Isolation valves are a means of providing isolation.
