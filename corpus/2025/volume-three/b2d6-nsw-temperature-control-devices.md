# B2D6 — Temperature control devices

**(1)** A temperature control device used to deliver heated water in accordance with B2D5(a) must be a—

(a) thermostatic mixing valve; or
(b) thermostatically controlled tap.

**(2)** A temperature control device used to deliver heated water in accordance with B2D5(b) must be a—

(a) thermostatic mixing valve; or
(b) thermostatically controlled tap; or
(c) tempering valve; or
(d) temperature limited water heater with a maximum delivery temperature of 50°C marked in accordance with AS 3498.

**(3)** The required maximum delivery temperature must be achieved in accordance with AS/NZS 3500.4.

> B2D6 only applies to sanitary fixtures for personal hygiene purposes—
>
> (a) When installing new heated water services, including extensions or additions to an existing heated water service; or
> (b) When replacing a hot water heater, regardless of the type or location of the heater.

> A temperature control device need not be installed if one or more, but not all water heaters in a bank of water heaters are replaced.
