# WA Introduction

Schedule 11 of the Plumbing Code of Australia (PCA) includes variations to the PCA as well as the referenced deemed-to-satisfy standards, AS/NZS 3500 Part 2 and Part 4. The Schedule includes variations to Schedule 1 that are common to all three volumes of the National Construction Code (NCC) but only relate to NCC Volumes One and Two.
