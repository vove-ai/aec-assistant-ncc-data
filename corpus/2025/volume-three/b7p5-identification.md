# B7P5 — Identification

Rainwater storage and holding tanks used only for non-drinking purposes must be clearly identified.

> (1) Identification is not required where rainwater storage is intended to supply water for drinking and personal hygiene purposes.
> (2) B7P5 does not apply to sites not provided with a drinking water service by a Network Utility Operator.
