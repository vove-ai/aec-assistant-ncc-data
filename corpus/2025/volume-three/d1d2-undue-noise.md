# D1D2 — Undue noise

To prevent undue noise, plumbing and drainage systems must comply with—

(a) B1D3 for cold water services.
(b) B2D9 for heated water services.
(c) B3D3 for non-drinking water services.
(d) B4D2 for fire-fighting water services.
(e) B6D2 for rainwater services.
(f) C1D3 for sanitary plumbing systems.
(g) C2D4 for sanitary drainage systems.
