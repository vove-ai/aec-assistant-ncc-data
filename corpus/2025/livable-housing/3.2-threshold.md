# 3.2 — Threshold

The threshold of an internal doorway that is subject to Clause 3.1 must—

(a) be level; or
(b) have a height not more than 5 mm if the lip is rounded or bevelled; or
(c) have a ramped threshold that—
  (i) does not extend beyond the depth of the door jamb; and
  (ii) has a gradient not steeper than 1:8; and
  (iii) is at least as wide as the minimum clear opening width of the doorway it serves.
