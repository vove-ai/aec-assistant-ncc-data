# 5.1 — Application

At least one shower must comply with Clause 5.2.

> ‘At least one shower’ means that in a dwelling with two or more showers, only one of the showers needs to comply with the requirements of this Part.
>
> A shower subject to this Part is not required to be located on the ground or entry level of the dwelling.
