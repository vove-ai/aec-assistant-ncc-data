# 1.2 — Parking space incorporated into step-free access path

(1) Where one or more car parking spaces are connected to or form part of a required access path, at least one of the car parking spaces must have—
  (a) a minimum unobstructed car parking space of 3200 mm wide x 5400 mm long; and
  (b) a gradient not steeper than 1:33 for bitumen, or 1:40 for any other surface material.
(2) For the purposes of (1), a required access path means an access path provided for the purposes of compliance with Clause 1.1.

> (1) Clause 1.2 only applies to a car parking space provided for the exclusive use of the occupants of the dwelling.
> (2) Clause 1.2 does not apply—
>   (a) if there are no car parking spaces provided for the exclusive use of the occupants of the dwelling; or
>   (b) to a Class 2 building.
